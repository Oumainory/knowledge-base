『 Game Information 』
〖 Location 〗
》 Post-Apocalyptic Metropolitan City
〖 Features 〗
》 Futuristic art style, distinctive characters & action-orientated combat system.
〖 Roles 〗
》 Contemporary civilization has been destroyed by a supernatural disaster known as the “Hollows”. They grow exponentially out of thin air, creating disordered dimensions where mysterious monsters dubbed the “Ethereal” roam. 
》 New Eridu, the last urban civilization that survived the apocalypse, managed to thrive by acquiring the technology to extract valuable resources out of the Hollows.
》 As New Eridu became the city of miracle and attracted more resettled residents, it also started the massive exploration of the Hollows for its perpetual expansion. The Hollows then became industrialized and monetized under the city’s *****istration, which gradually led to the increasing tension between monopolistic enterprises, gangs, conspirators, and fanatics.
》 Players in the game take on the role of a “Proxy” — a special professional who guides people in their exploration of Hollows. A great many people want to enter the Hollows for their own various reasons, and the “Proxies” are their indispensable partners. Players will help them explore the Hollows, battle their enemies, achieve their goals, and in the process learn more about their story.
