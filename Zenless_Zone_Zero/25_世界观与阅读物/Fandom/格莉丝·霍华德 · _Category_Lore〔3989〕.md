### 历史记录

^zzz-3989-20

> Grace Howard was born to two Hollow researchers who were more focused on their work than on their child. Their dedication to their research led them to forget to eat and to prepare food for their daughter. Despite this, Grace cherished the time they spent together, as her parents soon vanished while inside a Hollow. Now orphaned, Grace was taken in by Khors Belobog, a close friend of her parents. With her adoption, she became the adoptive sister to Koleda and formed a close bond with her, playfully calling her 'Sweet Pea,' whilst calling her new guardian 'Uncle Khors.' When Khors suddenly vanished one night and was accused with running with company funds, Grace was adamant that he was not guilty and continued to look up to him, while Koleda began to detest him.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3989-21

> With a strong interest in all things mechanical, Grace attended the Roose Institute of Technology to study and become a certified mechanic. Her obsession with machines led her to become antisocial with anyone organic, earning her the moniker 'The Frosty Cold Iron Witch,' or simply 'Iron Witch,' from her classmate/dormmate Betty Brenda. During her time there, her teacher commended her as his most exceptional student, adding, 'Nothing can hold you back.'〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3989-22

> Upon graduating, Grace returned home to work for Belobog Heavy Industries, now under Koleda's leadership. She began a large-scale project to create lighter, easier-to-mass-produce, and maintain In-Hollow Smart Machinery, using schematics of Khors' prototype as a base. However, her main goal was to create a 'child.' After enduring countless days, nights, and minor injuries, Grace nearly had the prototype working when the system failed to stabilize at the final moment. The following day, recalling her teacher's advice, she realised that Ether substance stimulation might be required for the system to fully stabilize. Taking her prototype into a Hollow, she successfully activated her 'child' just moments before Ethereals attacked; her new 'child' protected her from harm and was later named Greta. Grace went on to produce two other 'children', Friday and Hans.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-3989-25

> 格莉丝，白祇重工的技术专家，是将空洞内重工机械技术专利发展完善的核心人才。
> 醉心于齿轮、金属和电线，是当之无愧的「机械宅」。看到感兴趣的机械设备后会两眼放光，迫不及待想要拆卸研究：
> 「这冷峻硬朗的线条，真美啊…看得让人心痒痒的~」
> 
> 在面对机械时，格莉丝总是会表现出超凡的耐心，把这些精密的金属构成物当做自己的孩子般宠爱。
> 据相关信息判断，格莉丝是唯一敢喊珂蕾妲「小不点」却不会被她锤胫骨的人。
> 提问：在格莉丝的认知范围内，我是否也算是机械？但我目前并没有实体，因此也不存在齿轮，金属或电线之类的部件。
