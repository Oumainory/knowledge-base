### 历史记录

^zzz-25154-11

> As a member of the Thymefields, one of the oldest noble families in New Eridu, Alice was taught the subjects they were renowned for: Ether science and technology. Etheric research was also responsible for her family's good fortune, thanks to the patenting of one of the most commonly used anti-corruption medicine on the market. Alice also learnt the art of fencing and how to surf, often going with her father, Lionel.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-25154-12

> Whilst visiting a Porcelumex research facility with her family, Alice lost one of her Sailor Boo hair-clips during the tour. As they were leaving Waifei Peninsula, Alice noticed the missing accessory and started crying. Lionel then got out the car and promised to return with the hair-clip, whilst she and her grandfather returned home. Little did Alice know, that would be the last time she ever saw her father〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-25154-13

> Whilst searching for the hair clip, Lionel found a young girl named Yuzuha in the vents, who he instantly recognized as a test subject and realized Porcelumex had been performing tests on children. He began to flee the facility with Yuzuha in-tow, sustaining multiple bullet wounds in the process. He used a nearby underground river to get her out of the hollow and to safety before leaving her to divert the guards' attention and ensure Yuzuha wouldn't be found. Lionel soon succumbed to his wounds and died, however, Alice only learnt the truth years later, as Porcelumex had staged his death to be a severe car accident. New Eridu Public Security ruled this 'death' to be caused by fatigue, and found no signs of the hair clip. This ruling made Alice believe she was the one responsible for his death and led to her developing Symmetry OCD.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-25154-14

> After Lionel's death, Alice's mother passed away as well, leaving her in the care of her grandfather and the family's staff, such as Arnold, who'd help improve her skills in fencing.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-25154-15

> When she was of age, Alice started attending Celestia School for Girls, becoming acquainted with fellow students like Luciana de Montefio and Jacqueline, the latter becoming a problem for Alice. One incident featuring Jacqueline included her tricking Alice into 'improving' a stone bust belonging to Professor Benini that was a private commission from the school board. Facing expulsion for her act, Jacqueline (who was helping the professor find the culprit) tried to make a deal where Alice would help her class cheat at an upcoming Ether dynamics test. Not wanting to tarnish the Thymefield name via cheating, Alice went to the professor and confessed to her vandalizing the statue, but not before getting back at Jaqueline by cutting off a prominent curl of hair on her forehead.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-25154-16

> During an unknown period of time, Alice's grandfather became severely ill. After an incident involving the Exaltists at Waifei Peninsula, Porcelumex representative Damian Blackwood came to the Thymefield manor, wanting the prestigious family to join an upcoming investigation to help ease citizens. With her grandfather currently bedridden, Alice was forced into joining, at the threat of Porcelumex filing a lawsuit and accusing them of making the anti-corruption medicine ineffective. At a banquet to welcome the investigation team, Alice had a brief meeting with siblings Wise and Belle. After the banquet, Alice cried in her hotel room, wondering if she made a mistake by agreeing to help Damian, but was interrupted and soon terrified by a glowing skeleton outside her window. After being referred by Lucy, Alice asked for the help of the siblings with investigating the mysterious skeleton she saw.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-25154-23

> 爱丽丝·泰姆菲尔德，怪啖屋的新晋成员，新艾利都名门泰姆菲尔德家族的千金大小姐。
> 爱丽丝自幼修习击剑与以太科学，在以上领域表现出优秀的天赋。她当前就读于高志院下属的圣星女子学院，是一名品学兼优的标准优等生；推定，其有朝一日会继承家业，成为新艾利都新世代的以太学者。
> 她极为重视秩序与规律，尤其在事物的对称上表现出近乎偏执的追求。对称强迫症甚至对她的审美造成了影响，令她将事物对称与否置于判断其美丑的第一顺位。
> 此外，她极其容易受惊吓，对闹鬼怪谈的抗性几乎为0。即便如此，她似乎对接触怪谈表现出相当高的兴致。提议，主人可以寻找怪谈作为与她的共同话题，无论她前期怎么拒绝，推定最终都会凑过来一探究竟。
