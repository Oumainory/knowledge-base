Currently, all known tiger Thirens have a human-esque appearance, with tails and tiger ears, which are always coloured akin to a tiger with black stripes. ### 历史记录

^zzz-56336-2

> Tiger Thiren existed prior to the founding of Eridu, but established their own, independent settlement in the wake of the collapse of the Old Civilization. Recognising the power of Eridu's Void Hunter Weapons, they planned to create their own, culminating in the creation of the W-Engine known as the Tigerbane Bell, however the device was flawed in its construction. While intended to empower individual tiger Thirens by resonating with their ether frequencies, the bell required another tiger Thiren to be sacrificed to empower the user, and was also designed such that any non-tiger Thiren who used it would immediately become corrupted and mutate in to an Ethereal.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-56336-3

> The bell was seen as too dangerous and too cruel of a weapon, and so its use was forbidden and it was sealed away. Decades later, when Hollow disasters began to increase in frequency, the bell was brought up again, with the warrior Grim Tiger pledging to undertake a trial that, if she and her companions were successful, would prove that the tiger Thirens were strong enough without the bell, and it would remain sealed away. They were ultimately successful, but shortly thereafter the tiger Thiren's settlement was swallowed by a companion Hollow of Hollow Zero, as the chaos of the Fall of the Old Capital unfolded. 98% of all tiger Thirens perished, with the survivors being primary either young, elderly or sick individuls, who were evacuated in time to escape the danger. Despite this, the majority of those Thirens who died did so satisfied that they had kept their principles intact, rather than relying on sacrificing their own to survive.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
