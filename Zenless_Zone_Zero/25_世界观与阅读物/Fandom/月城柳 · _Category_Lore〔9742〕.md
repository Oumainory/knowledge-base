### 历史记录

^zzz-9742-7

> Tsukishiro Yanagi was a member of the New Eridu Defense Force Obsidian Division who served during the Oni rebellion. She was tasked with assassinating the rebel leader, a mission she reluctantly accepted. Whilst making her way to the leader's hideout, she met a young Soukaku and soon saved her from an oncoming missile strike, being captured by Oni rebels in the process. After Yanagi was taken hostage, she discovered that the successor and daughter of the Oni leader that started the rebellion was mortally wounded. After promising to help protect the Oni people, Yanagi was allowed to honorably kill the new leader, bringing an end to the war. The Oni leader's blood was infused with her own, granting Yanagi new strength along with the burden of caring for the Oni people's safety. Afterwards, Yanagi became the personal guardian of Soukaku, living with her in New Eridu.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-9742-8

> Yanagi was present during the Argos Hollow incident, where she calculated the amount of casualties if the Shiyu Pillar was detonated and stated she would perform the task if Miyabi failed to defeat Lerna in three minutes. After Miyabi's victory, she, alongside H.A.N.D. executive officer Asaba Harumasa, were transferred to the newly formed Hollow Special Operations Section 6 by the newly titled Void Hunter in order to expose the roots of corruption within New Eridu Yanagi later ascended to Deputy Chief, taking responsibility for both her ward and her fellow members.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-9742-16

> 月城柳，对空洞事务特别行动部第六课所属副课长兼情报官。
> 主要负责六课的人事管理、任务跟进与反馈、情报收集和现场支援等工作。
> 与此同时，月城柳还是六课成员苍角的「保护者」。除了在日常生活上对苍角进行照顾之外，柳还需要对苍角在工作中的一切行为负责。
> 
> 六课课长星见雅性格较为特殊，成员浅羽悠真总是过于散漫随性，成员苍角在心性上还是个大大咧咧的「孩子」——综合以上原因，六课绝大多数工作安排都落在了月城柳的肩上。
> 严谨认真，办事一丝不苟的月城柳，某种意义上算是六课唯一的「正常人」。也正是由于她的引导与指挥，才促使六课三名主要外勤队员的「武力」转变为「战斗力」。
> 整体来看，月城柳是一位综合能力超群，处理事物利落高效的优秀执行官。
> 但根据部分信息判断，月城柳在生活中似乎也会有迷糊的一面。
