### 历史记录

^zzz-7308-6

> Seed Sr. was a mass-produced military Intelligent Construct made by the New Eridu Defense Force (then known as just Seed), and, as was standard for these machines, had the defense system "Iron Order" installed into his logic core: a system that could override his personal autonomy to ensure he couldn't disobey orders. Veronica, a fellow Defense Force member, served as the medic for him and the other Intelligent Constructs in the military, and became close friends with Seed after he rescued her from a Hollow.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-7308-7

> Seed served faithfully as a soldier. He studied the methods of calculating Hollow frequency changes, which led him to try and prevent a show at the Ballet Twins after he had discerned the possibility of a Hollow appearing was exceedingly high. While this event would still lead to the Hollow's creation, he immediately worked to protect the civilians caught up in it, and afterwards helped the survivors fight for compensation. He also visited the Outer Ring several times, intervening to prevent Hollow Raiders from targeting the area's oil resources and fighting side by side with former Overlord Pompey.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-7308-8

> During the Fall of the Old Capital, Seed worked to find survivors, making seven trips into Hollow Zero and saving dozens of lives, including multiple residents of Waifei Peninsula. Amidst the chaos and ruins of the disaster, he found an orphaned girl, who had inexplicably managed to survive in the Hollow unassisted for half a month. The Iron Order program immediately perceived her as a "high-risk target" and made Seed activate self-defense measures to eliminate her. Still, he fought back against his systems, heavily damaging his logic core but overcoming the program and escorting the girl to safety. Before they left, Seed planted some seeds in the area where he met her.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-7308-9

> After returning from Hollow Zero, the orphan girl was inspected by Veronica, who noted her apparent lack of a "heart" as well as her unnaturally high aptitude for ether. She also found out that he had destroyed the self-defense system, which she stated was comparable to suicide, as the damage done would eventually cause him to shut down permanently, and there was no means of repairing him. Despite this, Seed was unfazed, and took it upon himself to take the girl in and named her Flora, teaching her about the ropes of life, to prove that both of them were capable of feeling and having a heart. He was also intent on finding out the mysteries behind her existence, as Flora had no memories of her time prior to Seed finding her. He began investigating the Fall of the Old Capital, believing that doing so was necessary to find out about Flora's past. Before he passed away, he promised Seed that he would return once she became stronger. One of his final thoughts around this time was wondering if the seeds he'd planted had grown into a field of flowers by now.<ref name="Animated Short Film"/> Before he completly shut down, Seed Sr. left a message inside himself, and entrusted a special data card to Veronica, which contained the coordinates in Hollow Zero where he first met Flora.<ref name="flora3"/>〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> The squad collectively agreed to tell Seed that he was currently on a special mission, and so couldn't currently be active or speak to her. Very shortly after Seed Sr.'s death, Obol Squad gained its final member, Soldier 11. As she had never meet Seed Sr., the rest of the team didn't explain to her what his actual situation was, but she immediately recognised how important he must have been to Seed. Due to this, when she found his phone still in the dorms, she began using it to text Seed as if she was Seed Sr. over the course of the next few years.<ref name="flora3"/>〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-7308-11

> After Obol Squad's confrontation with Isolde, Seed established a safe house in Hollow Zero as she contemplated methods to bring Seed Sr. back from his "sleep". The resulting events led to her confronting Veronica, and in the ensuing incident, she discovered that he was truly dead. Veronica gave the coordinates he had entrusted to her to Seed, which Phaethon and Fairy used to locate the spot in Hollow Zero where Seed and Seed Sr. had first met. Seed brought Seed Sr. to the monument, now surrounded by flowers that had bloomed in the 11 years since they were first planted, with her tears activating a mechanism in him that then played his final message to her, allowing her to finally process the grief of his death.<ref name="flora3"/>〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
