### 历史记录

^zzz-52630-7

> Remielle was one of the original generation of Void Hunters, having been born over 100 years ago, and is well known for pushing back the Dark Wall 37km, alongside Beliora Vike and the Leader of the Falkenhayn Mercenary Troupe. Before this she and Beliora were childhood friends, and Remielle herself was a well accomplished dancer, and described as a prodigy in most things she tried. She was close friends with the other Void Hunters, and was there when the Rosetta Data was proven to be viable, and when Sunbringer began prototyping the original Bangboo, where the rabbit ears she proposed eventually made it in to the final design (though most of her designs were rejected).〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-8

> After having worked together with the other Void Hunters to take down an Enchainer, Remielle became one of the recipients of the Void Hunter Weapons that were forged from the beings Karma Core, that being Thaumiel, and used its power to great effect alongside her contemporaries. However the weapons all had severe side effects due to their power stemming from The Creator, and so when Arche and Sunbringer began their Project Sunseeker, which aimed to merge the Void Hunters with their weapon's Karma Cores, with the hopes of mitigating the costs while becoming even stronger, she joined the effort alongside Beliora, however this ended tragically as Beliora would transform in to an Enchainer, and Remielle was forced to kill her.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-9

> After this, Remielle's own transformation in to an Enchainer began, and the results, though incomplete, locked her in to a temporal stasis, where she would remain for the next century.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-10

> ----〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-11

> About one year before Phaethon would arrive in Roscaelifer, Remielle awoke from her stasis, and promptly sealed Thaumiel away. Over this period she tried to grow accustomed to the new world, and investigated what happened to the other Void Hunters, leading her to fly to Roscaelifer to find Sunbringer. She encountered Eldreda, and confronted her about what happened during Project Sunseeker, but ultimately left without recieving an answer. It was then that she realised that she would need the power of Thaumiel if she wanted to press forward with her goals, while this "attempted assassination" made her a wanted criminal in the city. At some point in time she joined the Covenant of Dayat, though it is unclear if this was immediately after she was freed from stasis, or sometime after.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-12

> Following the Creator's defeat at the hands of Ye Shunguang, Remielle assumed the identity of "Ramiel", and went to retrieve her weapon Thaumiel, while cooperating with an unknown third party. At some point her actions became known to Public Security and Krampus, as the faded Ethereals left in her wake were discovered by them, prompting independent investigations by both parties. She entered the Hollow Champion Competition and first met Phaethon while they were examining the prizes, namely the Flint Porcelloy W-Engine - "Concealed Light". At the time, she stated that she was unsure what prize she should aim for, but had noted Phaethon's fixation on the W-Engine. In truth, she needed the W-Engine, as it was the key to unsealing her weapon.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-13

> Throughout the competition, she made use of her powers to clear through Ethereals with ease, but wouldn't show her face to Phaethon again until after they had passed out and suffered from a strange nightmare. Phaethon left Random Play to get some air, running in to Ramiel in front of Godfinger. She inquired about them, asking about their favorite Foodie Fighters character, and asking for help in fixing their Ethereal card, with Phaethon answering and helping her despite not intending to. She explained that she now intended to go after the Porcelloy W-Engine, before cryptically saying she would "pay you back for this favor by dealing with your nightmare in the future." Refusing to answer any follow-up questions from Phaethon, she promptly left.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-14

> When Romeul stole the W-Engine, along with the rest of the prize pool for the competition, Ramiel pursued him in to the Hollow, coming in to contact with several of Romeul's cohorts in the process. She dislocated every bone in their bodies before moving on after finding out they didn't have the W-Engine with them. After Romeul was defeated by Phaethon and their companions, she used her powers to freeze them all in place, casually taking the W-Engine from Phaethon's grasp, but not before noting that they seemed to just barely resist her abilities. She warned them to never let her get that close again before disappearing.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-15

> Phaethon, Billy, and Cissia would pursue Remielle following this, tracking her through Lumina Square where she had encounters with Coco, Yi, Hailey, and Orchidea, before heading to Blazewood. She encountered Phaethon again, dispelling their visions and answering a few more questions, while also revealing her true identity, to the group's surprise. Alongside Promeia, the group would follow her towards her weapon, with Rusty Cliff sending forces to try and dispatch her as well. This eventually led to Phaethon coming in contact with Thaumiel, which transformed them in to an etheric entity of incredible power that immediately attacked their friends. The transformed Phaethon singlehandedly defeated Billy, Promeia, and Cissia before Remielle intervened, reversing the transformation and apologizing for getting them in this situation. She explained she has a connection to Helios Academy like them, and that a doctor in Roscaelifer would be able to heal their condition before putting them to sleep.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-17

> With Thaumiel now back in her possession, Remielle was able to sense the three Karma Core fragments that had been distributed among Eldreda, Dracaene and Lindverne, and came to understand their true nature. She came in to contact with the En-Nah Gang, with the ancient Prophetboo recognizing her as the real Remielle, and convincing the gang to shelter her. Under Griff's orders the Bangboo created a makeshift home for Remielle in their base at the Retroflux Zone, which remains her current base of operations.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-18

> After Phaethon arrived in Roscaelifer, they and Norma snuck in to the CCD as they were investigating a case where Bangboo were being assaulted and killed. At this time, Remielle continued her investigation, but lacking any idea on how to operate the area's computers, accidentally triggered the alarms, causing a brigade of Bangboo to chase Phaethon and Norma right to her. The three agreed to continue on together despite her criminal status. They discovered a Singularity Leakage in the area being caused by the Ethereal Girtablullu, and hunted it down to rescue the imperilled Bangboo Toby, but before they could finish it off, Bashmu appeared, and the group was forced to retreat. Eventually Remielle was able to use her powers to suppress the monster, while Phaethon (with help from Helios), pushed it back in to a Fissure, successfully escaping.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-19

> Afterwards, Remielle snuck in to Phaethon's room at Amber Hall, and after some back and forth, explained the concept of Enchainers and Karma Cores to them, before coordinating a new meeting with them later. At the appointed time, Remielle appeared in a Mr. Roar costume, but before she could converse much with Phaethon, Velina appeared and she had to retreat. She returned to the En-Nah Gang's hideout, which eventually was found by Phaethon, and she agreed to help in their search for more information of the culprit behind the Bangboo-killings. The revelation that the culprit looked remarkably like their old friend from Helios Academy - Serpico, led Phaethon go investigate a mech as it was about to explode, forcing her to intervene as the two had a heated confrontation. Due to this however, the two resonated, each recieving a glimpse of the other's memories. In truth, Remielle had experienced this before, as this resonance had occured when she had dispelled their transformation some time before, but Phaethon's lack of conciousness at the time meant this was their first time seeing these memories.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-20

> The two talked about the past, before Phaethon returned home to try and discern the truth about the culprit's motives, with Remielle later finding the devestated Phaethon and sheltered them from the rain, escorting them back to her home where they then helped to dry her wings as they confided in one another, with Phaethon revealing both their findings and that Dracaene had attacked them, with Remielle realising it must have been Girtablullu, disguised as Dracaene - the Enchainer larvae, intent on finalizing its transformation, was planning to absorb Dracaene fully and become a full on Enchainer. During this time, Sigrid unwittingly found them at the hideout, having been on orders from the possessed Dracaene to locate them. Sigrid came to coordinate with them to resolve the situation, culminating in her smuggling the pair in to Roswick while wearing Mr. Roar costumes.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-21

> They coordinated a plan to take down Girtablullu, with Sigrid creating an opening at the banquet that was being held for the guests arriving from New Eridu. After sharing a dance with Phaethon, Remielle burst on the scene and used her powers to freeze everyone in place and take Dracaene through a Singularity Leakage, far away so Girtablullu could be dispatched, though she paralyzed Phaethon as well, understanding that they had a full life to live, while she had nothing and no one left, removed from time as she was. However, Phaethon broke free of this and pursued her, resolving to defeat the monster together. After a harsh battle, Phaethon, Pyrois and Remielle were victorious, killing Girtablullu and rescuing Dracaene, though Bashmu returned to consume the dead Ethereal and take the Karma Core piece for itself.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-52630-22

> While trying to stabilize Dracaene's condition, Remielle's Enchainer transformation began to surface, and as the Airspace Patrol arrived to take Dracaene, Remielle accepted all charges, with her and Sigrid trying to absolve Phaethon of any wrongdoing, but Phaethon resisted, and used their power with Pyrois to escape the scene, where they then angrily confronted her for doing too much on her own. After taking some time to recover from the transformation, she returned to Phaethon, and it was revealed that she had begun cooperating with Airspace Patrol, and was planning to head to Vyrium along with Dracaene, as the Porcelloy there would help stabilize both of their conditions. She also explained how the Sunbringers were effectively living Void Hunter Weapons, though she knew this was a lie that Phaethon would eventually uncover the truth about on their own.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-52630-25

> 蕾米埃尔·丹，初代虚狩之一，曾经的节杖军中校，秘密组织达识结社的成员。
> 据记载，蕾米埃尔自幼便展现出极高的天赋，能够迅速掌握大多数领域的知识与技能。
> 她年纪轻轻就被选拔加入节杖军，破格晋升为中校，继承了虚狩武器「塔乌弥尔」，并在与维克上校、法金汉佣兵团团长的协同作战中斥退黑墙、一战成名，顺利授勋虚狩。
> 在史称「静默之日」的机要行动中，蕾米埃尔与维克上校因意外双双殉职，但既然她尚存于世，那便说明真相并非如此。
> 蕾米埃尔来去无踪，却总能在出人意料的关键时刻出现，引导局势。
> 与人交际时，蕾米埃尔始终带着甜美而游刃有余的微笑，处变不惊、甚至有些自来熟，很难让人起戒心。
> 她擅长用稍带玩笑意味的言行迅速拉近关系，但又习惯保持一种恰到好处的距离感，令人难以捉摸。
> 备注：蕾米埃尔喜欢用一些无伤大雅的玩笑捉弄他人，而在这方面，您显然享受着格外的「优待」。

^zzz-52630-28

> 「静默之日」行动中，蕾米埃尔与维克上校参与了由亚契和挽昼提出的，名为「逐日计划」的实验。
> 逐日计划试图将虚狩与虚狩武器中封存的「业核」进行融合，人为创造结使，以消除虚狩武器对持有者施加的代价，让虚狩能以更强大的姿态保卫艾利都。
> 试验的结果是失败的，两名虚狩全部折损，所幸在塔乌弥尔力量的影响下，蕾米埃尔并未完全转化为结使，而是陷入了百余年的沉睡。
> 自珀塞纳斯空洞苏醒后，蕾米埃尔所熟知的时代早已落幕，她与达识结社的成员接触，并选择加入结社。
> 然而，蕾米埃尔的结使化并未终止，不仅如此，过度使用虚狩武器将加剧这一进程。
> 当侵蚀到达临界点，她的体表将浮现可怖的结使化症状，而她似乎极度抗拒让任何人看到那副模样。
