> As the captain of Lyre Squad, Charon brought a shy rookie sniper named Phlegethon on board. Despite the sniper's codename maintaining stylistic consistency with the rest of the squad, it was deemed rather cumbersome to pronounce, and Charon later changed her codename to Trigger. Over the span of a thousand days, Charon taught Trigger how to play the harmonica, watched hone her combat skills, and grow from a shy kid who blushed whenever someone spoke to a skilled sniper that cared for all her squadmates, albeit a little too much. Charon noticed how each time one of them got hurt, Trigger seemed to be on the verge of breaking down, and believed the squad's motto "Having each other's backs" was becoming a burden for the sniper. In an audio message she recorded with the rest of Lyre Squad, Charon told Trigger to not bear everything alone and to start relying and fully entrusting her vulnerable side to them. She ended the message by playing a song she had composed for the squad as a gift to the sniper, her masterpiece: "Homecoming."
