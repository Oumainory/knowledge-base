### 历史记录

^zzz-17287-8

> The once shy Phlegethon was a soldier brought into Eridu Defense Force's Chrysoberyl Division - Lyre Squad by its captain Charon to be their sniper. Her codename was considered cumbersome by Charon, who decided to redub her with a new codename: Trigger. Over the span of a two and a half years, Trigger became more talkative and skilled as a sniper, capable of adjusting to changes in wind speeds on the fly and protecting her teammates from harm. She also made tight bonds with each member of Lyre Squad to the point her captain noticed how she seemed on the verge of breaking any time a member got hurt. Whilst recording a message with the rest of Lyre Squad to celebrate Trigger's 1000th day, she told the sniper not to bear everything alone and to trust them with her vulnerable side and gifted her a song she composed on a harmonica "Homecoming". Trigger would never hear this message until after the old capital's fall.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-17287-9

> When Hollow Zero first began expansion, Defense Force HQ believed it was a normal Hollow disaster that could easily be stopped with the standard amount of troops, this included Lyre Squad, who were sent with the promise of reinforcements coming to support them, but soon what they expected to be a regular amount of Ethereals became an onslaught and their backup never came. Trigger watched her fellow soldiers die or be corrupted until she was hit with a powerful etheric beam to her eyes, which corrupted them to the point that 90% of her eyesight was gone. Trigger soon lost consciousness due to that and other wounds, and the last thing she ever saw was her squadmates, heavily corrupted and injured yet still protecting her.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-17287-10

> Trigger soon left the Defense Force, now suffering heavy PTSD from the Hollow Zero incident. Over time she learnt that her disability had granted her a unique ability to detect etheric fluctuations of humans, Ethereals and objects within a Hollow, an ability she named "Ether Vision". During this time Trigger spent most of her time around Port Elpis. It was at this same location where she heard the familiar melody of "Homecoming" once again.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-17287-17

> 「扳机」，新艾利都防卫军·黑曜石营·奥波勒斯小队的狙击手。「扳机」是其于艾利都时期入伍所持有的代号，一直沿用至今。
> 
> 有着极其特殊的双眼，虽然无法看到绝大部分事物，但在空洞环境下可以清晰辨别一切具备以太波动事物的方位与变化。注意：具备以太波动的事物，泛指生命体、原生以太物质、以太制品以及以骸。
> 
> 作为广义上的「盲人」，「扳机」将视觉以外的五感锻炼到了极致，因此可以如正常人般活动，甚至敏锐地洞悉周遭环境的一切变化。这使她高度适配狙击手的战术定位，并可胜任潜行、监视、暗杀等一系列隐秘任务。
> 
> 不同于精准而致命的战斗表现，她的性格相当无害。除了有时会悄无声息地出现在录像店内、对第二助手造成突然的惊吓以外，基本可以认为不存在任何危险性。
