> Big Pan served as a member of the Melt Iron Mercenaries. During the time of Eridu's founding, various mercenary groups were tasked with aiding in efforts to claim territory for the city to expand, which the Melt Iron mercs helped with. Big Pan was already an incredible swordmaster by this time, but this only caused him concern, as the ease by which he could dispatch his enemies, and the thrill it brought to him, led him to worry that he might become a monster. He often panicked in the face of combat, unwilling to engage enemies. This attitude led to the other mercs believing him to be a coward, and at one stage he threw away his sword and ran during a conflict with bandits. Ultimately however, he was able to use this fear to hone himself, as it was the very thing that helped him from becoming that monster he despised, and returned to protect his fellow mercs.
