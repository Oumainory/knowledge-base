> Lucius first appeared in the start of Chapter 4, when he and Mors tried to stop Phaethon and Billy Kid from reaching Blazewood by forcing their truck to drive into a Hollow. The two were rescued by Caesar King, though none of them realized Lucius' involvement.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> Prior to the Tour de Inferno race, Lucius tried to sabotage the Sons of Calydon on multiple occasions, including by directing Bellum to destroy their Hollow data beacons and hiring Pulchra to steal the Sons of Calydon's parts. The Sons of Calydon also discovered that Mors was smuggling listening devices into goods sent to Blazewood, again under Lucius' orders. Though Pompey quickly put a stop to the sabotage, Mors claimed he acted alone.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-13122-4

> Later, Lucius planned to use the Tour de Inferno race to extinguish Cinder Lake, which would destroy the Old Oil Field's deposits and make the region dependent on Ether technology. In the process, he hoped to assassinate both Pompey and the leaders of the rival gang Sons of Calydon, making himself the undisputed ruler of the Outer Ring. Lucius and Mors both joined Pompey during Tour de Inferno race, where they competed with the Sons of Calydon to re-ignite Cinder Lake. Lucius had Mors blow up the Sons of Calydon's part of the track, hoping to bury them in rubble; he then tricked Pompey into throwing a fake sparkstone, containing an 'Ether Aggregation Catalyst' into Cinder Lake, causing it to become rapidly corrupted. Realizing Lucius' betrayal, Pompey confronted and wounded him. However, the rising Ether levels quickly incapacitated Pompey, as Lucius had lowered his Ether aptitude with a form of poison. While Pompey was slowly corrupted, Lucius proclaimed that he didn't care about his ideals of freedom and morality, saying that with the power of Ether, he would create a new order with himself in control.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-13122-5

> Lucius retreated upon discovering that the Sons of Calydon had survived the blast. However, he left Pompey alive, hoping his corrupted remains would finish off the arriving Sons of Calydon. Once Caesar, Phaethon, and the other Sons of Calydon made it to Cinder Lake, they found Pompey, who with the last of his energy told them of Lucius's betrayal, before becoming a powerful Ethereal. He was soon defeated by them. Caesar then heroically dived into the center of Cinder Lake to re-ignite it with her working Sparkstone.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
