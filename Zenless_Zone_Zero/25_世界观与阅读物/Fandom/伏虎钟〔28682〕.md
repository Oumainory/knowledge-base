### 历史记录

^zzz-28682-3

> The Tigerbane Bell was a weapon created by an ancestor of the Tiger Thiren Clan in order to replicate the power of the Void Hunter Weapons, however it was flawed in a way that those weapons weren't, requiring the user to devour other Tiger Thirens in order to use it. Due to its cannibalistic and cruel nature, the bell was labeled as a "forbidden relic" and barred from use. Not long before the Fall of the Old Capital, there was an increase in Hollow related disasters around Eridu. The tiger Thirens often went in and out of the Hollows, making their lives much more dangerous. With the safety of their clan at risk, the subject of the bell was brought up again.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

Grim Tiger was ultimately successful, and it was agreed that the bell should remain forbidden.
