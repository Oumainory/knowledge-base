### 历史记录

^zzz-40149-8

> Banyue was originally created by the old civilization and was later developed by TOPS as "Purge Unit - Type Zero". His purpose was to destroy Miasma, and with his algorithms built to work towards the most efficient method of Miasma disposal at all times, he was ruthless in his work, leading to destroy ethereals and afflicted civilians in equal measure. A failed operation left Banyue broken within the Lemnian Hollow, abandoned by TOPS. Despite this, he was found by a "sage" from Marcel Group, who rewrote the programming within his Core to function like a Logic Core. He was later retrieved from the Hollow by Ning Shu, who brought him back to Failume Heights.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-40149-9

> During this time that he began to develop a sense of self, eventually becoming a fully sapient being. He spent a great deal of time with the residents of a mining area near the Lemnian Hollow, becoming friends with many people there, but ten years ago, the Lemnian Hollow swallowed the area. Banyue's original programming in regards to Miasma affected him during this time: his logic algorithms determined that there were only a handful with a chance of survival at the time, so he chose the optimal solution and did not try to save anyone else. He had however saved Ye Shiyuan and Ye Shunguang after being tasked to do so by their parents. which he proceeded to do, though the school was never named; years later, Ning Qian joined it as a student. He introduced himself to them, and immediately began to help in investigating the mysterious town that had appeared in the Lemnian Hollow after the Miasma began receding. He cooperated with Zhao of the Krampus Compliance Authority, even going so far as to allow her to look in to his operation logs if the information within could help their investigation,〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-40149-10

> Unbeknownst to Banyue, Krampus had been working to find and retrieve the Purge Unit - Type Zero, since Banyue never took the Forbidden Fruit Test, he was considered a rogue machine with no humanity: a valuable missing asset at best, a danger to others at worst. After Dialyn arrived in Failume Heights and the situation in Timesworn Hills was resolved, Krampus began looking into Banyue further, with the intent of claiming his core by whatever means were available. With the help of Phaethon and Shunguang, she helped him escape the array, and through the voices of the dead, revealed the truth about what happened ten years ago, causing Ning Qian to break down and apologize for his actions. Though Banyue accepted his death, and was willing to offer his core to Dialyn, the Judge recognized his personhood and offered him the punishment of living with his guilt and pushing forward in life, rather than dying. He then joined her, Shunguang and Phaethon as they went to confront Sarah and Shiyuan, coming face to face with a Shiyuan mind-controlled by the Creator. During the battle, Banyue shielded Dialyn and Phaethon from harm, but ultimately had to be rescued by Shunguang using the power of the Qingming Sword to defeat her possessed brother. It is implied that after overcoming his sense of guilt and joining Krampus, he took and passed the Forbidden Fruit test, getting thus recognized as construct citizen.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-40149-13

> 般岳，一位在卫非地开设武馆、门下学徒众多的智能构造体，存活于世已有很长时间，人称「般岳师父」。他有着如石狮子般威严的外表，武艺高强，与云岿山现任门主仪玄有着多年的交情。
> 
> 作为武术高手，般岳寡言而可靠，行事风格与观念古朴，对门下习武的学徒严以管教，是因材施教、授人以渔的武学宗师。他有着名为「怒相」的战斗特殊形态，一旦开启，可将眼前邪祟尽皆诛伏。
> 
> 作为长期居住于卫非地的普通居民，平日的般岳则性格温和沉稳，不善情感表达，言辞举止颇有古人之风。他自知跟不上人类时尚潮流的发展，私下正学习着各类网络新潮用语中，然而因为理解不到位，时常会造成一些让人哭笑不得的误会。
> 
> 此外，般岳似乎很喜欢石墩子等球状物体。若与其独处因其沉默而陷入冷场，推荐以球状物来唤起其好奇心。

### Close

^zzz-40149-16

> Banyue's true identity is that of a killing machine secretly developed years ago by TOPS, specifically designed to suppress Miasma and codenamed "Purge Unit - Type Zero." The algorithm implanted within him drives him to act according to the most logical solution for eliminating Miasma, which often results in indiscriminate annihilation of all moving targets within sight, including both the Miasma and affected civilians.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-40149-17

> After a failed operation, Banyue, then still a mere machine, was abandoned by TOPS, but was then discovered by a sage visiting the Lemnian Hollow, who rewrote his Core and constructed new intelligent logic for him, allowing Banyue to gradually develop self-awareness. Later, Banyue was accepted by the residents of the old mining site in Waifei Peninsula, where he integrated into "human" life.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-40149-18

> During the catastrophic Miasma outbreak at the old mining site ten years ago, Banyue rushed to rescue the civilians, yet under the influence of his perfectly rational algorithms, he abandoned those he deemed beyond saving. Though he saved the young Ye Shunguang and Ye Shiyuan, this planted a deep-seated guilt within him for "abandoning those in need." As a result, Banyue never took the Forbidden Fruit test, and despite meeting the criteria of an Intelligent Construct, he remains officially unrecognized as one.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-40149-19

> After receiving judgment from Dialyn, a Judge of the Krampus Compliance Authority, Banyue resolved to atone for his past regrets by protecting the future. Through Dialyn's recommendation, he has now become a Judge of the Krampus Compliance Authority, embarking on a completely new journey.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
