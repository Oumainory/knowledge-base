> When she was young, she always dreamed of becoming an actress. However, she ended up landing a job at the White Waves Hospital instead, taking care of newborns as a nurse. She rose as a dark horse through the competition, making it all the way to the finals. On the same day as the finals though, a Hollow engulfed the hospital she worked at. She eventually made it to the hospital, searching through the rubble and saving as many babies as she could.
