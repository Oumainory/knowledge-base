> At some point, the Black Anchor began making several unidentifiable transactions within the Hollow, the details and participants of which were highly classified. These activities were recorded by Bangboo Merchants, creating the risk of scandal should they be leaked to the public. As such, the gang opted to conduct an operation within the Hollow to wipe out all the Bangboo: the original plan to take out the specific Bangboo with the recording had to be changed due to the Hollow's complex nature making it impossible to find. To that end, the gang posed as normal clients on Inter-Knot and arranged for a rookie Proxy to guide them to the merchants, as hiring a simple rookie would make them easier to kill afterwards.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

With the gang coming under pressure, they tracked down Venus and began threatening her to reveal the Proxy's location to them.
