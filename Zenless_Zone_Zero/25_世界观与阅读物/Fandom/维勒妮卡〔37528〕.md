> Veronica joined the Defense Force sometime before the Fall of the Old Capital. During her time in the military she served as a medic for many Intelligent Constructs who served at the time. These were mass-produced Intelligent Constructs, and as such they all had the Iron-Order defense system installed in to their logic cores, something Veronica, along with many others in the Defense Force, found particularly egregious. She befriended many of her patients, one in particular being Seed Sr., who had rescued her the first time she had ever been lost in a Hollow. Before his final shutdown, he entrusted her with a data card that contained the coordinates for where he had first met Seed, though he didn't explain why.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-37528-3

> Many of the Intelligent Constructs that served the Defense Force eventually died over their course of their tenure, with Veronica grieving for them. Higher ups within the Defense Force began a plan to repurpose the bodies of these individuals by installing new logic cores in to them, which caused Veronica to find a way to stop this, with a plan to work with the rebels and, hopefully, figure out a way to revive her fallen comrades. She hoped to get help from Seed, whom she hadn't spoken to since before Seed Sr.'s death, but after seeing Seed had no true conception of death, she refused to work with her, instead using Hollow Raiders to steal various military mechs that would then help move the Intelligent Constructs bodies to safety. Her plan was interrupted by Seed and Phaethon, and in a freak accident caused by one of the Hollow Raiders; Heric, Seed had to fight off a Miasma Corrupted Terror Raptor, and in the chaos of the fight the Intelligent Construct's remains were heavily damaged with many being destroyed. Veronica confronted Seed about this, with Seed's newfound realization of the finality of death causing her strange power to become unstable, causing what remained of the constructs to self immolate.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

Following this, despite the crimes she had commited, she recieved support from a "conservative mechanist faction" who agreed with her course of action, and thus avoided being imprisoned.
