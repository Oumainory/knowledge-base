### 历史记录

^zzz-21500-8

> Ju Fufu lived in the tiger Thiren settlement as a child, and grew up watching old martial arts films, being inspired by them, particular the tiger Thiren actor named Cang Huang, and the fictional tiger Thiren heroine Hu Weiwei.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-21500-9

> During the Fall of the Old Capital, a companion Hollow of Hollow Zero consumed the tiger Thiren settlement, and the powerful Ethereals who appeared at the time decimated the residents, with 98% of them being killed. Yunkui Summit appeared shortly after the Hollow emerged, and Ju Fufu was rescued by the efforts of the mystics of Yunkui Summit, being one of the few survivors of the now near-extinct tiger Thiren clan. As Yunkui Summit made a deal with Porcelumex to leave Failume Heights following these events, Ju Fufu would join Yunkui Summit and lived on the mountain proper with what remained of the mystic order, being the first disciple of the new Grandmaster: Yixuan.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-21500-10

> She maintained her training over the years, and eventually would come to have juniors in Ye Shiyuan and Pan Yinhu. At some point in time, Ju Fufu would encounter Hu Wei, who would become her companion and combat aid from then on.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-21500-11

> Several days before the events of Where Clouds Embrace the Dawn, Yunkui Summit began their return to Failume Heights, resettling within the long abandoned Suibian Temple. Once Phaethon joined the ranks of Yunkui Summit, Ju Fufu became quick friends with them and supported them in their efforts as the most senior disciple.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-21500-12

> Ju Fufu was roped in to a movie production called Legend of the Tiger Warrior!, however it was revealed this was a plot by The Exaltists to use the Tigerbane Bell; a tiger Thiren artifact long thought lost, to steal the power of Ju Fufu and one "Master Tiger", a process which would have turned them both in to ethereals. This however was revealed to have been orchestrated by Master Tiger; he had used the bell multiple times on his own kind since the tiger Thiren's decimation, and had manipulated the Exaltists in to drawing Ju Fufu out so he could do the same to her. Using the bell he merged the gathered Exaltists in to a powerful Ethereal with the hopes of defeating her, but Ju Fufu defeated it singlehandedly before apprehending the Master Tiger and handing him over to Public Security.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

Records show that multiple notorious Ethereals appeared in this Hollow that swallowed up their settlement, killing 98 percent of all registered tiger Thirens. Most of the survivors were the sick, young, or elderly. If it hadn't been for a team of mystics from Yunkui Summit arriving within half an hour for search and rescue, the name Ju Fufu would likely have never appeared in your Agent records.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
