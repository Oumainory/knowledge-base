> Upon the launch of the game in Version 1.0, the vast majority of H.D.D.-based Story Commissions and Exploration Commissions, alongside Hollow Zero: Withered Domain, featured "Array Mode", which refers to exploring Hollows through the "TV Screens", moving tile-by-tile and encountering various Hollow Events. In Version 1.2, it was decided that Array Mode would no longer feature in Story Commissions going forward, and would later be replaced by something else. In Version 1.4, the vast majority of pre-existing Story Commissions were reworked, with Array Mode being replaced in favour of "Marcel Maze" gameplay (aka "Eous Gameplay").〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
