### 历史记录

^zzz-3988-5

> The earliest known history of Von Lycaon's life was his time as an apprentice of Jack, an aging phantom thief who performed acts of thievery with the goal of making the world just. Sometime during his tutelage, he and Jack came across Hugo, fresh off his escape from the abuse of the machiavellian Ravenlock Family whom he shared blood with. Jack would take the young man under his wing, teaching and feeding both of them. The three would go on heists together, the two students forging a tight yet friendly rivalry with one another. Over time, Lycaon realised that Hugo learnt quicker than he did, but despite the minor advantage, he felt confident he could always overpower him.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3988-6

> One night, Lycaon was called upon by Jack to speak in private. Jack had been suffering from an unknown disease, and although he still continued to go on missions with his students, his illness was about to take his life. Before his death, Jack told Lycaon about a "seed of evil" Hugo had inherited from his Ravenlock lineage. Fearing his student would go down a path of evil, Jack ordered the thiren to keep an eye on Hugo, and if the seed was ever to take root that he should "hold his chain tight, to restrain him, to stop him." However, unbeknowst to both at the time, Hugo had overheard the entire conversation, the topic causing the noble to feel confused about himself.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3988-7

> Jack would peacefully pass away in his sleep, leaving orders for his Students to follow upon his death, with his body being buried somewhere in the Outer Ring. At his grave, Lycaon and Hugo decided to form their own phantom thieves guild, dubbing it Mockingbird, at Hugo's suggestion. Though no longer a student, Lycaon would find himself being mentored by Hugo on how to act like a dramatic phantom thief, with him having to recite lines that he deemed "cheesy" and "edgy."〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3988-8

> Over time, Mockingbird gained both fame and infamy for their criminal acts of justice. During this time the duo would also adopt a Bangboo named Robin to assist in their heists. Their reputation reached a point where Mayor Mayflower took action; he made contact with the guild and offered them an olive branch - to work for him in exchange for being forgiven of their crimes. Though it's unclear what Lycaon's response was at first, if any, the wolf thiren would later take up Mayflower's offer after one fateful mission...〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3988-9

> What was once an ordinary heist turned into a massacre as a Hollow opened and took the life of countless people. He found his partner-in-crime surrounded by the bodies of his estranged family, including his father. Lycaon quickly came to the belief that Hugo had planned this entire heist all around killing, not knowing the truth of Hugo's hesitation and Hartman's responsibility for the deaths until years later. Feeling betrayed by Hugo, who had broken Jack's golden rule of never killing, Lycaon proclaimed his distaste for the bastard noble and how he will take up Mayflower's offer. He stayed in the Hollow to aid the innocent lives trapped within, but at great cost: his right eye and both legs.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3988-10

> Despite his physical disabilites, Von Lycaon kept to the deal and begun working for the Mayflower Family. He was given advanced cybernetic prosthetic legs so he could walk again and took the position of head butler and leader of Victoria Housekeeping Co. The rough wolf thiren was able to quickly adapt to his new role, becoming an elegant gentleman who could perform any task asked of him with ease.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3988-11

> One day, when he and his team were tasked with acting as security personnel for the hollow-consumed Ballet Twins, they found the infamous odd-job company Cunning Hares and Legendary Proxy Phaethon tresspassing within the building. Lycaon was prepared to deal with the unwanted guests, but was quick to change his tune when he learnt that the Cunning Hares's newest hire, Nekomiya Mana, and Phaethon were responsible for helping Corin Wickes get out of the Dead End Hollow after being seperated from the group. Both Von Lycaon and Alexandrina Sebastiane already knew of Phaethon, including their knowledge about Sacrifices, as both were tasked by Mayor Mayflower with spying on the two siblings and to lend them aid when needed.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3988-12

> Von Lycaon would later assist Phaethon in rescuing Rain from the top of Ballet Twins and save countless citizens (plus the Cunning Hares and Perlman) from an Exaltist plot that involved crashing the blimp they were on into the Ballet Twins. He would keep in contact with the two siblings, befriending them over time.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3988-13

> Although Lycaon had rid himself of his past life, it would come back to haunt him on two seperate ocasions. The first being when Lilac, the daughter of a family he was acting as a butler for, desired to join Mockingbird and almost fell prey to a group of thugs imitating the group, though Lycaon quickly dealt with them and showed off a flair for drama he had gained from his former partner. The second time had him cross paths with Hugo Vlad once again, though both fought at first they soon worked together to stop Hartman Ravenlock and The Exaltists' plots. Lycaon aided Hugo in a multi-layered deception that involved the latter feigning a betrayal against Phaethon and company to side with the Ravenlocks, Lycaon 'killing' Hugo then helped him overcome the effects of a poison Hartman had given him, and finally both of them dealing the final blow to the Ravenlock's name. With their plan completed, Hugo told Lycaon the truth about overhearing he and Jack's final conversation and also what truly happened on that fateful night.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-3988-16

> 莱卡恩，维多利亚家政的实质领导者兼对外负责人，负责统筹管理所有成员的工作。
> 理性又可靠，举止优雅绅士，如同万能执事一般，不管发生什么事情都能妥善解决。是团队最坚实的后盾，令人心安的存在。
> 有些轻微的洁癖，总会习惯性地把周围清理干净，难以忍受脏污的环境。
> 
> 虽然莱卡恩看起来总是波澜不惊的优雅模样，但偶尔也会因为犬系的生理本能暴露本心。当他特别高兴的时候，尾巴和耳朵总会无意识地摆动。当事人似乎也清楚的知晓这一点，并为此感到困扰。
> 除此之外，如同大多数毛茸茸的希人一样，莱卡恩很在意自己的毛发状况。
>
