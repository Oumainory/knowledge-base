> The first known Drowned Ideal was created eight years ago when Ah-Deh, a member of the Defense Force's special unit, suffered corruption in an empty town in Timesworn Hills. As he was being corrupted into an Ethereal, his desire to spend more time with his daughter created the Drowned Ideal Little Lucia, also known as "Lantern Bearer." Ah-Deh became the Ethereal known as the Wandering Hunter soon after and formed a unique bond with Little Lucia, the latter regarding their interactions together as a game of hide-and-seek.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> Two weeks prior to the events of Season 2 Chapter 4, Yidhari Murphy was brought to the town by Sarah, and Yidhari used her ability to recreate past illusions of the town; while doing so, she was sedated by Sarah and the Miasma's influence made her subconsciously attach her desire to "watch others' stories" to the town. Thus, the town itself became Yidhari's Drowned Ideal. Two days later, a group of trapped miners arrived to the town and became Drowned Ideals, while their physical bodies were enveloped in Miasmic cocoons. The next day, an Exaltist named Viggo Moss came to the town to meet with Sarah and help her sedate Yidhari. Moss eventually formed his own Drowned Ideal, regarding it to be his real self and calling his physical body a "shell." As more people arrived, Moss took note of their Drowned Ideals and conducted experiments. He cut into one of the cocoons, and while the person inside quickly became an Ethereal, their Drowned Ideal still existed. However, when Moss killed the Ethereal to prevent it from attacking the other cocoons, that Drowned Ideal vanished forever. Moss kept this a secret from the other Drowned Ideals and only told them that they must protect the cocoons.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
