> During the Fall of the Old Capital, there was a Miasma outbreak at the mining site: Shiyuan and Shunguang's parents managed to protect the two children with a technique, while they themselves got corrupted, and asked Banyue to leave them behind and save the kids instead. This experience scarred Shiyuan, who started having nightmares of his parents getting killed by a monstrous figure in the Miasma. After a short period where the two siblings had to scrape by in a relief center, Banyue brought them to Yunkui Summit so that they could be taken in under Yixuan's guidance. At first, the news made him ecstatic, but when he learnt of the curse the sword entailed, he swore to find a way to save Shunguang, even resorting to experimenting with forbidden techniques, trying to shift the Qingming Sword's side effects onto himself instead. This angered and saddened Yixuan, and he promised he would never let his obsession ruin him, not even for Shunguang's sake.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
