### 历史记录

^zzz-17236-8

> Outer-Ring born, Pulchra Fellini was raised in one of the poorest towns in the wilds. Her only friend was a fellow Thiren named Milena, who she'd played with often. Their most played game was pretending they lived in the city, which included roleplaying as waiters and customers. The two Thiren shared a dream of one day living in the city and truly being the people they pretended to be, but living in the Outer-Ring and having notable Ether Aptitudes meant the best career for them was becoming mercenaries. Despite not knowing their Ether Aptitude levels, both Pulchra and Melina became mercenaries, after a while Melina suggested that she and Pulchra should start their own group and swiftly put Pulchra in charge, using her bank account for it.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-17236-9

> Sometime after forming the group, Pulchra realized that the goods a client entrusted them with have gone missing, alongside all the money in the account. Confronting Milena about this, the fellow mercenary revealed she took the funds in order to start a new life in the city, before knocking her out. Though she spared Pulchra's life, she left her with a massive debt that she decided to run from, leaving others to pay the price for their trust.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-17236-10

> Pulchra went back to being another mercenary for hire, only thinking about herself and her personal gain over bonds and trust. It was during this time she was contacted by Lucius of The Vanquishers with a job offer.  Wanting to sabotage the Sons of Calydon chances of partaking in the Tour de Inferno, the scheming second-in-command hired her to acquire parts the rival gang had ordered, a task she did whilst pretending to be a member of them.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-17236-11

> Expecting the Sons of Calydon to come, she set up explosives in order to ambush them, she managed to get Burnice caught in the explosion, but was soon caught off-guard herself by the human's eccentric personality, granting enough time for her fellow members to catch up. After a small battle, Pulchra, recognizing her opponents' strength, surrendered, offering to hand over all the vehicle parts in exchange for them keeping quiet about the confrontation. Burnice accepted Pulchra's offer and invited her to join the Sons of Calydon, though Pulchra refused and waited to see if they are the strongest in the Outer Ring. After another battle, she surrendered again and left the bikers to fend for themselves, finally considering the Sons of Calydon to be the strongest.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-17236-12

> Sometime after Caesar's rise to Overlord, with few job offers and her income becoming unstable, Pulchra learnt that the Sons of Calydon were looking for a capable intelligence-gather, seeing an opportunity, Pulchra agreed to join and became a member of the gang. Though her past run-ins with them are considered to be in the past, Pulchra was placed on probation, requiring to complete several errands, later revealed to be a way of welcoming her to the gang. Though she deemed them a bunch of idiots, she was happy to have friends once more.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-17236-15

> 波可娜，原外环雇佣兵，现为卡吕冬之子成员，物流公司猪突猛进挂职职位：普通员工。
> 警告，检测到外环多个机车族阵营和不法帮派与波可娜存在过资金往来，在加入卡吕冬之子后，此类记录有所减少，但来自新艾利都的资金转入记录大量增加，推测是由于波可娜的新身份能更方便地出入新艾利都。
> 此外有资料表明，波可娜与数起外环重大事件存在间接关联，包括并不限于：帮派火并，车队重要物资情报泄露，道路离奇损毁，重要物资涨价…
> 波可娜并未与卢修斯有过深的联系，目前也不存在身为其他机车族阵营内应的风险，但她的人际关系极为复杂，建议与其接触时提高警惕，尤其注意保护个人隐私。
