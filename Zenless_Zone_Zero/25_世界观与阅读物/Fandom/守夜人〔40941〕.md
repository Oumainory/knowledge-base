> Long ago, the ancestors of the Nightwatchers once fought a powerful Ethereal known as the Night Horror for days on end. Right before the Night Horror fell, it released a surge of power, which cursed the Nightwatchers to dream of the Night Horror when sleeping at night. This would make them sleepwalk into Hollows, regaining consciousness only for a brief moment before they turned into Ethereals. However, it also gave them ability to be perceived by Ethereals as their kin, and thus be unbothered by them.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> Another version of the legend states that the tribe deliberately hunted down the Night Horror to seize its power of controlling other Ethereals. When the Night Horror unleashed its final surge of energy, half of the tribe immediately turned into Ethereals, and the other half was forced to defeat them. This sacrifice of their own people was the only reason they were acknowledged by the Hollow and received the gift-curse.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

Though the Nightwatchers ultimately survived the catastrophe, they suffered numerous casualties. Years later, when Phaethon first met Lucia, a Nightwatcher named Kieran had plans to betray the tribe, having grown embittered due to the discrimination he faced due to being part of the tribe.
