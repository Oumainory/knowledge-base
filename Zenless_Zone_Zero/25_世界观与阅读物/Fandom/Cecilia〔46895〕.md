### 历史记录

^zzz-46895-2

> Cecilia was a famous singer and song-writer who worked under the name "Cersea", but she suffered from PTSD following the Fall of the Old Capital, which made her unable to sing despite the numerous songs she wrote at the time. Her agency forced her to continue her work, as her singing could help alleviate the hopelessness felt by the people following the catastrophe. They found a voice double for her, the Intelligent Construct Aria, who had been awoken from her slumber in a Hollow due to hearing Cecilia's music, and they began working together. Aria stood in as Cecilia's voice for her last few shows before she retired from performing, but she felt terrible about the situation, and believed that Aria should be able to succeed on her own, without being stuck in her shadow.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-46895-3

> As time went on, Cecilia recieved treatment for her PTSD, Aria's singing helped her recover. She also became friends with Astra Yao at some point. Her appearance and voice changed, and she eventually came to meet the Angels of Delusion, which Aria was part of. She signed on to be their vocal coach, though due to her lingering guilt over having Aria work as her voice double, she withheld her former identity as Cersea from the group. She also came to take over 404 Error, offering invitations to various groups such as Satellite, The Forecasters, The Darkforge and The Deepsea Aliens.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
