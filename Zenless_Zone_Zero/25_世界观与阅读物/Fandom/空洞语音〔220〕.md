### 环境

^zzz-220-18

> thumb|The interior of a Hollow.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-220-19

> The once-ordinary interiors of Hollows are drastically altered by Ether, turning them into mazes of distorted environments. Within Hollows, normal laws of physics and spacetime don't apply; a seemingly-straightforward path could be riddled with invisible fissures and blockages that lead to random locations, while what looks like a dead end could contain a portal out of the Hollow. Some Hollows even seem to be interconnected — passing through a fissure in one Hollow may lead one into an entirely different Hollow. Communication with the outside world is nearly impossible from within a Hollow, with only the legendary Proxy Phaethon having the means to reliably do so, the Hollow Deep Dive System. Additionally, the environment within a Hollow shifts frequently, meaning one path through a Hollow could change, making conventional cartographic methods useless in charting the interior of a Hollow.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-220-20

> Hollows are naturally complex places, and being careless can lead to certain death for inexperienced and experienced explorers alike. To safely explore Hollows, many explorers use Bangboo, small robots equipped with Carrot programs. These programs create maps of Hollows by predicting the behavior of space within the Hollow, using data collected from past observation of Hollows. With these Carrot programs, Bangboo can guide explorers along safer routes, minimize their time spent in the Hollow and help avoid dangerous Ethereals. Alternatively, Fairy is designed to actively analyze Hollows by drawing upon the computing power of nearly 80% of all smart devices around New Eridu, allowing it to map out Hollows in real-time without the need for Hollow observation data.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
