### 历史记录

^zzz-3976-8

> Before the Fall of the Old Capital, Anby was known as Soldier 0, the captain of the Silver Squad, a group within the New Eridu Defense Force Obsidian Division. Unlike other squads, Silver Squad's infantry consisted of replicas, artificial humans made via genetic cloning. This made them incredibly loyal and powerful (albeit very high-maintenance) soldiers that could be easily replaced if they died in battle. As Soldier 0, Anby was considered the best of all the replicas, obedient to the point where she would kill herself if ordered.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3976-9

> All replicas considered themselves to be sisters and cared for each other as family. Therefore, when the Defense Force tried to select only one replica to keep and then dispose of the others to cut maintenance costs, Anby started failing her tests on purpose to convince the Obsidian Division that the entire Silver Squad was needed. One day, their superiors decided to devise a suicide mission to kill off all members of the Silver Squad and keep Anby as the sole replica. However, the night before the operation, Harin, known now as Soldier 11, got injured during training, so Anby took her place for deployment.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3976-10

> Despite the mission being created ad hoc to leave no soldier alive, Anby managed to survive and return to the base after witnessing the death of her sisters. Upon her return, her disgruntled superiors, who wanted to keep only one replica alive, gave Anby the order to kill Soldier 11. In an act of disobedience towards her superiors and of love towards her sister, Anby slit her own throat instead, so that the Obsidian Division would be forced to keep Soldier 11 as the sole replica. Her superiors hence kept Soldier 11 and disposed of Anby's body by leaving it by the ruins of the Old Capital, where she was eventually found by Nicole Demara,〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-3976-14

> 安比，狡兔屋成立最初的雇员（没有之一）。
> 根据新艾利都居民档案可知，「安比·德玛拉」这一身份在其加入狡兔屋后才凭空出现，且「德玛拉」的姓氏明显取自妮可。而在以狡兔屋雇员的身份进行相关登记之前，安比的个人履历及相关数据全部呈现可疑的空白。
> 安比虽然极度缺乏常识，但却精通战斗相关事宜，属于狡兔屋最强战力（之一）。
> 
> 安比最大的兴趣爱好是电影，且涉猎范围极其广泛。或许是因为过度痴迷，她常把电影中的故事当真。
> 和看起来一副喜怒不形于色的模样不同，安比很容易被一些并不存在的故事情节感动。
> 最钟意的食物是汉堡，因为「同时包含蛋白质，碳水和绿叶菜，味道好吃，甚至还价格低廉的食物，除了汉堡之外没有其他。」
