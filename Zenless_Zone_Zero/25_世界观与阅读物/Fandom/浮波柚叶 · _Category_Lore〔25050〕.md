### 历史记录

^zzz-25050-7

> "Project New Leaf" was an infamous and inhumane experiment founded by Porcelumex co-CEO Ferox, who kept it a secret from Lucro and TOPS. Countless children were swept up from the streets of New Eridu and subjected to experiments with the hopes that corrupted antibodies found in their blood would host suitable catalysts for Porcelume purification and solve a bottleneck in the process the company was having. Due to the intensity of such experiments and the lab being located inside the Lemnian Hollow, every child-subject died, all except for ES-07.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-25050-8

> Originally an orphan found in the Faunus Quarter, ES-07 had no real name - except for the nickname "Little Candle," an alias given to her by a gang of street kids due to malnutrition-induced night blindness that led to her walking about with a candlestick. Lacking a real name, one of the low-level researchers assigned to her care decided to give her one, Yuzuha. Yuzuha was lucky to have survived the experiments for so long and was theorised to have PTSD from becoming the sole survivor. However, this wouldn't be the case, as Yuzuha hid a playful personality from the employees of Porcelumex.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-25050-9

> One day, after her final injection for the day, Yuzuha decided to partake in one of the new sources of joy she had found: climbing into a vent found in her cell to sneak into a showroom lab and eavesdrop on all sorts of interesting things. As fate would have it, she happened to witness Lionel of the prestigious Thymefield Family, who was touring the facility with his daughter: Alice. Yuzuha was able to catch one of Alice's Sailor Boo hairpins that had fallen off of her hair before it hit the floor, before then falling unconscious due to a higher than normal dosage administered earlier. When she awoke, she was found by Lionel, who had returned to find Alice's hairpin. Having learned Porcelumex was conducting human experiments, Lionel rescued Yuzuha and helped her escape via an underground river. He told her he'd be right behind her, but instead got as far away as possible to make sure Porcelumex wouldn't find Yuzuha and eventually was shot multiple times by security and lost his life.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-25050-10

> Washing up on the shore of Sailume Bay, Yuzuha was found by Li Baorong, owner of Mystic Wares and a man who lost his wife and two daughters during the fall of the Old Capital. Though having adopted a boy prior, Baorong took the girl in. Despite her name being given to her by one of the researchers, she decided to introduce herself as Yuzuha to him, as she felt the name held a spark of life, said spark being part of her luck. Having learned her name, Baorong noted how weird it would be to call her "Li Yuzuha," to the point he wouldn't be able to forgive himself for giving her such a ridiculous name, and thus decided to give her a new last name based on her story: Ukinami.  The name Yuzuha would later inspire the tradition of Li naming his later adopted children after plants.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-25050-11

> Yuzuha would grow up and start attending a low-tuition company-affiliated school located in the city, being classmates with Komano Manato, and also help out at her father's store, Mystic Wares. She would also rescue a tanuki that was being drowned by a group of children, which would then help Yuzuha out during a struggle in a Hollow. The two became inseparable ever since, with the girl naming him Kamanosuke. She also modified the Sailor Boo hairpin into a repair tool, considering it her good luck charm.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-25050-12

> Sometime prior to the events of Season 2, Yuzuha became a member of Spook Shack, an Inter-Knot subforum dedicated to supernatural stories and investigation. She would write up a story and forge a scam with the assistance of Kama to sell "Sacrifice Repellent" amulets in relation to the recent disappearance of Justin Bringer. Yuzuha would later have a fateful reunion with Alice after she fell victim to one of Kama's pranks and asked members of Suibian Temple Phaethon for help. This reunion led to Yuzuha confronting her past as a test subject, exposing the crimes of Ferox, and revealing she was indirectly responsible for the death of Alice's father. She would later be saved by her new friends with the help of Yixuan and promised to live on together with Alice, her new best friend.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-25050-15

> 浮波柚叶，卫非地澄辉坪居民，目前就读于市内一所学费低廉的企业校，除了绳网子论坛「怪啖屋」之外并未加入任何其他组织——当然，按照新艾利都的一般认知，网络论坛并不能被视作正式的组织，更应被称为同好会。
> 柚叶是辉瓷工艺品店释诡斋店主黎宝荣的养女。根据澄辉坪流传的小道消息，宝荣叔在旧都陷落时失去了妻子和两个女儿。旧都陷落后，他先后收养了五个孩子，柚叶排行第二，有一个哥哥，一个弟弟和两个妹妹。柚叶家位于店铺所在的公寓楼二楼，由于家中只有两间卧室，柚叶一直和两个妹妹共用一个房间，男孩们则和宝荣叔住在一起。最近，由于哥哥已离家独立，柚叶也大多数时间住在学校宿舍，所以家里显得不那么拥挤了。但周末和假期，柚叶一般会从学校返回澄辉坪，帮助父亲打理店铺。
> 柚叶从小就有着与年龄不相符的成熟，因为能说会道、性格开朗，深受邻里喜爱，在澄辉坪和泅珑围的小孩子中间也很受欢迎。
> 柚叶在怪啖屋论坛使用的网名是「柚子胡椒」，在论坛上交好的除了现实中认识的真斗和爱丽丝之外，还有网名是「夜魔使者」和「草莓芭菲」的两位用户。

### Close

^zzz-25050-18

> 据柚叶所说，她从记事起就一直在法努斯区流浪，生活在一个由数个孩子组成的流浪帮派中。儿时的柚叶没有名字，因为营养不良患有夜盲症，晚上总要端着烛台，因此外号叫做「小蜡烛」。
> 柚叶参与的实验项目名为「新叶计划」，由于她一直没有正式称呼，某个负责照顾她的研究人员给她起名「柚叶」，但除了这些日常接触实验儿童起居的底层研究人员之外，菲洛克斯和其他研究所上层并不知道这个名字存在，始终以ES-07来称呼她。虽然是在实验室获得的名字，柚叶却始终觉得，这个名字中含有的生命力，也是自己幸运的一部分。
> 柚叶的两个妹妹都是在婴儿时期被收养，宝荣叔在给她们起名字时也延续了使用植物意象的习惯。
