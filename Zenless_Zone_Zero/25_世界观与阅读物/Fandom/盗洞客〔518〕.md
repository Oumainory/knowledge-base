### Description

^zzz-518-0

> Hollow Raiders are not a unified organization per se, although some Hollow Raiders are known to work together in groups. The term "Hollow Raider" refers to any person who enters a Hollow illegally and/or without the proper authorization. Thus, some characters and playable Agents are considered Hollow Raiders, such as the Cunning Hares. Hollow Raiders use Inter-Knot for communication, an online service which acts as a forum as well as a place to post commissions.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-518-1

> Most Hollow Raiders look for treasure in the Hollows, such as Ether resources, or complete commissions for payment. Navigating the Hollows requires the use of a Bangboo that is modified to recognize Carrots, which are time-sensitive data used to map a safe route through the Hollow. Hollow Raiders usually acquire Carrots distributed by Proxies, and often work alongside a Proxy to complete commissions.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-518-2

> The majority of Hollow Raiders have an Ether Aptitude scoring above 50, which allows them to survive in the Hollows for some time without suffering corruption. Most Hollow Raiders are armed and prepared for combat, mainly against Ethereals. Among other Hollow resources, they also seek out Gear Coins to purchase useful supplies from Bangboo merchants deployed within the Hollows.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
