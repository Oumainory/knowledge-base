> Papago Hollow was first navigated by Joyous, who was alive during the era of Eridu's founding. By going in with only "Sunbringer"'s prototype Bangboo, Joyous was able to mitigate some of the Hollow's effects, and fully navigated it. During this time Joyous and Sunbringer worked with Omond Flint and Deryn Ata on the construction of Omond Research Station - a facility made primarily out of Flint Porcelloy that could serve as a stable base of operations to investigate Papago Hollow from within. Due to the way that the Station affected the Ether in the area, creating a donut-shaped space around it, this would be called "Operation Bagel".
