### Biology

^zzz-4141-1

> Thirens are a race of Anthropomorphism beings, known for their unique blend of human and animalistic features. Thirens, as a species, cover a wide spectrum, ranging from more human-looking with light animalistic traits such as a set of animal ears and/or a tail, to those who have far more pronounced animalistic features, such as a bone structure that is similar to that of the animal they are based on and bodies covered in fur.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-4141-2

> Thirens have a naturally high resistance to Ether. However, this aptitude functions like a bell curve, where if they undergo Corruption, it overtakes them faster than humans, and often damages their fur given corruption's tendency to affect hair quite quickly. Thirens exhibit superhuman abilities such as immense strength; for example, Ellen Joe's tail can break bones if she is not careful. Thirens often share some features with the animals they resemble, which can both positively and negatively impact them. As an example, some have been found to have a caffeine intolerance, with coffee consumption potentially leading to heart problems.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 历史记录

^zzz-4141-5

> While the origins of Thirens are unknown, they have existed since before the founding of Eridu, as when space exploration was prevalant, bear Thirens were noted to have been among those cosmonauts who ventured in to space. Multiple Thiren became part of the city's population upon its founding, such the Void Hunter "Swordmaster" along with the rest of the Hoshimi Family, and the giant panda Thiren Big Pan, while other groups such as the tiger Thirens chose to establish their own civilizations and live seperately from Eridu. Thiren have always made significant contributions to society, with multiple instances of high profile Thirens as part of the upper eschelons of society and in scientific fields.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-4141-6

> In New Eridu, Thirens are recognized as citizens by default, and are found in all walks of life. While some Thiren groups such as bear and feline Thirens are common, others are quite rare, such as tiger, giant panda and turtle Thirens, as not all Thiren groups have equal populations to one another. Though Thirens are capable of being incorporated in to human families, this seems to be a rare occurence, as Komano Manato's adoptive siblings, A-Yuet and A-Cing, were noteworthy enough that Manato had to explain the situation to Phaethon when they first encountered them. Currently, almost all known families with Thirens in them are entirely made up of Thirens, and all of the same type.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
