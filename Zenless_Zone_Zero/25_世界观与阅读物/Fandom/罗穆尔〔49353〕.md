> Romeul had been sentenced to death for unspecified crimes before the Fall of the Old Capital, but escaped during it as Hollows ravaged the city. He drifted to the Outer Ring and began working as a Sweeper, his job being to dispatch anyone he was ordered to, which he took great pleasure in. He earned the title "Black Wolf" during this time, but wasn't fully satisfied with his life as a Sweeper, as it didn't fully sate his bloodthirst. > When the new Hollow Champion Competition began shortly after Gilded Carrot Day, Romeul was approached by Rusty Cliff Corp., the primary sponsors of the competition. They were using the Flint Porcelloy W-Engine - "Concealed Light" as "bait" and intended for Romeul to thin out the contestants within the competition by subduing them, making it more likely that their intended target would be caught out. He took the opportunity immediately, working alongside various criminals from the Outer Ring, including Mors, but the wolf Thiren's bloodthirst meant he was only content with killing his targets rather than subduing them as he was contracted. He was eventually confronted by Phaethon, Cissia and Billy Kid, the latter of whom had brought the competitions organizers, forcing Romeul to stand down.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> Following this, Romeul was contacted by Rusty Cliff, who severed ties and informed him that they would facilitate his escape, but nothing else. Enraged, Romeul demanded to talk to an unknown person high up in Rusty Cliff, but was simply told that he was dissappointed in him, and that nothing further would be done. Romeul quickly deduced that this "escape" they intended for him - being escorted out of New Eridu via Port Elpis, was almost certainly their way of getting rid of him by killing him at sea and dumping his body. He resolved to have his revenge by disrupting their plans as much as possible, breaking in to the competition grounds, stealing all of the possible prizes, including the W-Engine, and spreading them throughout a Hollow, hoping to gather as many people as possible within to serve as a smokescreen for him so he could escape. He contacted several other criminals from the Outer Ring such as Hedgehog and Ghostshade to assist him before releasing a video to draw people in.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
