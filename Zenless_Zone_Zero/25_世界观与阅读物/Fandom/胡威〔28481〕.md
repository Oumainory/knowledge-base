> Hurcules was created by Samson Hu on the 2nd of April. He considered it to be his greatest creation, and designed the Golden Mecha for Hurcules to ride, based on the comic "When Aliens Came to Earth". He also designed a backup mecha for it. After Samson Hu was killed in a corporate power struggle, the Golden Bangboo was left devestated, having only the mecha and the comic to its name as its possessions. Now resentful of humanity, it likened itself to being a "destroyer of humanity" as the Bangboo in the comic did. Despite this, Hurcules didn't know how to active the mecha's full potential, and so was unable to use it to exact any kind of revenge.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> Twenty years later, Hurcules lost access to the Golden Mecha 01. The Bangboo caused a disruption in Lumina Square after losing the Mecha, forcing Public Security to intervene, but it was able to make its way to Brant Street Construction Site to confront Belobog Heavy Industries and the Cunning Hares, who had retrieved the mecha and were trying to disable its defense systems, both to control it and to prevent it self destructing. By the time Hurcules arrived, Anton Ivanov had already contacted Phaethon, who with the help of Eous, had been working to operate within the mech's protocols so that it wouldn't explode.
