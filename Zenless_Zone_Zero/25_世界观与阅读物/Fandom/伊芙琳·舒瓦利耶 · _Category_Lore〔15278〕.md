### 历史记录

^zzz-15278-6

> Evelyn's childhood is largely unknown, and she remembers quite little of it, though she does recall growing up by the seaside with fondness. At some point during her youth, she was brought in to the "organization" and trained as an assassin/spy. The "organization's" recruits were not given much free time beyond their training, leaving Evelyn envious of other children living freely throughout the city.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15278-7

> While Evelyn was still a child, and stumbled upon the final performance of Joran de Winter, unknowingly being there at the same time as a young Astra.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15278-8

> Evelyn performed numerous jobs for the "organization", using numerous pseudonyms such as Anna, Flying Squirrel and Lily, and sustaining numerous injuries in her long combat career. Rain also worked in the "organization", and became indebted to Evelyn after she saved Rain's life at an unspecified time in their past. The final job given to her was to work as a spy for the company Fugue under the identity of Scheele's Green. The mission entailed infiltrating their rival company Odeum HEA, acting as a mole by becoming the manager/bodyguard of Astra Yao. After being prompted by the man who assigned her missions, she opted to use her real name rather than a code name for this job.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15278-9

> The mission began two years prior to the events of Astra-nomical Moment. During this time, Evelyn was warmed by Astra's sincerity as a person, opening herself up and growing significantly. She learned to cook during this time to take care of the both of them, and eventually cut ties with the "organization" altogether, choosing to work faithfully as Astra's manager.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15278-10

> This however earned the ire of Fugue, who considered her to be a traitor, while members of Odeum would also become suspicious of her in turn. This would come to a head during Astra's new years performance at Starloop, where Tessa de Winter plotted to make the two corporations come to blows while she tried to destroy the tower, causing mass destruction and heavily damaging both company's reputations in the process. Evelyn collaborated with Phaethon to stop the crisis, and protected Astra throughout, going so far as to suffer a gunshot for her safety, before the situation was resolved.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15278-11

> She would recover from her injuries and after divulging information about the "organisation" to New Eridu Public Security, returned to her duties as Astra's manager. Despite this, elements of Evelyn's past remain active and aware of her current situation; she has recieved cryptic messages from those who know about her prior identities.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> A razor-sharp weapon crafted to meet Evelyn's particular professional needs. Made from strong, tough Porcelume fibers and linked at the tip to a brilliantly sharp Porcelume blade. This weapon is lightweight and discreet, making it easy to carry, while also being capable of bypassing security checks focused on uncovering more commonplace weapons. While capable of functioning as a noose, under special circumstances, the Lunalux Garrote can be set ablaze along its entire length. Its ingenious design ensures every strike is clean and precise. Much like Evelyn herself, the Lunalux Garrote is both restrained yet sharp, elegant yet deadly.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-15278-17

> 伊芙琳是丽都第一歌姬耀嘉音的经纪人兼贴身保镖，以其冷静果断的决策力和专业干练的风格闻名。是嘉音背后无可取代的支柱，「说到商业价值，耀嘉音如果是那个源头的1，那伊芙琳就是跟在后面的全部的0。」某娱乐杂志记者这样评论道。
> 
> 既能规划周全的行程和代言，又能在各种突发事件中保障嘉音的安全。「想要接近嘉音？要先过伊芙琳 · 舒瓦利耶那一关才行…」尽管工作繁忙，伊芙琳从不显得慌乱。她对细节有着近乎苛刻的要求，无论是嘉音的每一场演出，还是代言产品的品质，都必须经过她的严格把关。但奇怪的是，如此优秀的经纪人，却并不受到其母公司帝高娱乐的信任。
> 
> 情报：伊芙琳的身份似乎不仅仅是经纪人兼贴身保镖这么简单，背后似乎有一个「组织」。
> 
> 嘉音的核心加饭群里，嗑这一对CP的人似乎相当多。
