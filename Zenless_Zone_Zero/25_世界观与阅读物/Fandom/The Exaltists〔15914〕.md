### 历史记录

^zzz-15914-6

> It is unknown when The Exaltist church was founded, but it is safe to assume it was after the first Hollow disasters began occurring. One of their leaders, the , was taken down by "Swordmaster" immediately prior the founding of Eridu. It is reported that they were publicly active as a religious group before the Fall of the Old Capital, holding rallies in the open; however, it is unknown why they went undercover in New Eridu.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15914-7

> The Hollow Zero disaster seems to be tied to the Exaltists, as someone attempted to retrieve the Sacrifice sealed inside the monument at Pioneer's Memorial Plaza the night before the Fall. On the same night Carole Arna was abducted by one of the Creator's hands while the Helios Academy was under siege by mysterious armed groups and "monsters". Moreover, their chanting can be heard inside Hollow Zero.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15914-8

> After the death of her comrades from Agamemnon Squad during the Fall of the Old Capital, Isolde started investigating, eventually joining the Exaltists under the name Mevorakh to gather more information. adopted Vivian Banshee and treated her like her own daughter, due to her gift of foreseeing calamities. When Vivian shed tears in front of an Exaltist member, they were claimed to be "blessed" and got injected with the serum: those who transformed in Ethereals were taken down on the spot, while the successful Sacrifices  were given to Exaltist higher-ups; however, the low success rate, together with the fact Landon's Sacrifices did not hold human consciousness, brought the higher-ups to see his serum as a failure, with Landon gradually losing influence within the Exaltists. At some point, Vivian cried in front of Dina, Landon's own daughter. In a gamble to try to regain the favour of the higher-ups, Landon decided to inject Dina with the serum. To try to save her adopted sister, Vivian told everyone present that Landon should be the one to receive the "blessing," and forcibly shed tears in front of him. Landon tried to kill Vivian, but was stopped by Camille, and in the altercation he accidentally shot himself. Dina blamed Vivian for her father's death and succeeded him as the head of the sect, while many lost and uncertain followers, including Vivian, left.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15914-9

> At the same time, a now-Exaltist official Mevorakh came to Janus Quarter and relocated many of Landon's followers in Waifei Peninsula, where they struck a deal with Ferox, Porcelumex co-CEO, who would have provided the Exaltists with funding and shelter in exchange of the tech obtained from Sacrifice research. When the previous Overseer died, Mevorakh got chosen as their successor, and immediately publicly declared that the Creator would not mold unwilling subjects, thus rejecting Porcelumex's proposal to carry human experiments similar to Project New Leaf.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
