> Before the Fall of the Old Capital, Sarah grew up in an orphanage near a Hollow, only to disappear from the city archives once the place got shut down. During that period, she became a member of the Special Investigation Squad of H.A.N.D.'s Hollow Special Operations (HSO). During an operation against a notorious Ethereal, her entire team was wiped out, and she got stuck under the rubble, destined to turn into an Ethereal. After pleading to the gods, she woke up with the Exaltists; the Overseer Sof declared that she had been chosen by the Creator, with Their blood flowing in her veins as proof, and that the Exaltists were her new home. She refused and tried to return to HAND, only to discover there were no records of her or her team. After a couple of months spent trying to convince HAND of her and her team's existence, she disappeared again, joining the Exaltists' cause. allowed her to climb the Exaltists' ranks, becoming a key leader, second only to the Overseer.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> In order to carry out the plan of developing an effective Sacrifice serum, she orchestrated several Exaltist operations together with Justin Bringer, including the infiltration of Vision Corporation to retrieve the Sacrifice sample from inside the Hollow, the manipulation of Charles Perlman, the Null_Face attack against Section 6 during the VR test, and the hacking of the Hoshimi family by White Noise. After luring Miyabi into their trap, she personally extracted the power necessary for Bringer's refinement from Tailless, and later shot Bringer with the final dose of Elixir P-07 to complete his transformation. She then relocated to Waifei Peninsula, where the Exaltist stronghold was, to aid Overseer Mevorakh in her plan. She and the Overseer had struck a deal: when Mevorakh's revenge plan came to fruition, she would open the path to the Creator for Sarah. She picked up Dr. Lohr's call for help in the Overseer's stead, convincing him to self-sacrifice. When the cultists turned to her after Mevorakh's apparent defeat against Yixuan, she calmed them down and welcomed the Overseer back from the Miasma coagulum.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
