### Biology

^zzz-13025-1

> Intelligent Constructs are defined as machines with Logic Cores who can pass the "Forbidden Fruit Test", and thus showcase true personhood and free will. While many robots in New Eridu have Logic Cores that grant them some degree of intelligence, such as Bangboo and the construction machines of Belobog Heavy Industries, not all of them are Intelligent Constructs. An Intelligent Construct's memories can be accessed via their "operation logs". Any recordings made by an individual can be accessed here, but it is largely considered to be a breach of privacy to do so. Constructs are usually highly resistant to efforts to tamper with the code of their Logic Cores, as Fairy stated she was entirely incapable of taking control of a construct like she can with Bangboo, however when under emotional duress, Youkai was able to corrupt their emotions, causing them to behave more erratically, and even hijack their bodies entirely.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-13025-2

> Intelligent Constructs have artificial bodies with a variety of physical characteristics. As what defines a construct has to do with their Logic Core, they come in a wide variety of forms, from the nearly human-looking Qingyi to more humanoid-like individuals such as Elfy and Old Duyi, to entirely non-humanoid existences like the car Sleip and the popcorn cannon Hu Wei. Intelligent Constructs can customize their bodies more readily than humans or Thirens. They also demonstrate much higher Ether aptitude than the average person.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-13025-3

> Some Intelligent Constructs have body parts which are analogous to body parts in humans, Thirens or animals, but serve different purposes or have different functionality, such as Qingyi's ponytails being Nano Static Eliminators that she can remove and have replaced or repaired, or Banyue's tail, which is prehensile, unlike the tails of actual cats or feline Thirens. Due to being mechanical entities, Intelligent Constructs sometimes run into unexpected problems during daily life, such as being so heavy that they can't swim, causing them to always sink in bodies of water and requiring specialist equipment to get them out, or activating metal detectors unintentionally.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-13025-4

> Intelligent Constructs have no need for human sustenance, but in some cases can still consume it if they want to, though they need to be modified in such a way as to allow for this. They can't consume most liquids, as such many places such as Richard Teamilk and Coff Cafe provide various flavours of motor oil for them to drink instead. They need to charge themselves in order to stay active, and will shut down if they run out of energy, only reactivating within the proximity of a charging station. Constructs are generally more precise and capable than humans, and as long as their Logic Core keeps running, they can theoretically live almost indefinitely, as evidenced by Qingyi and Billy Kid, who were created in the era before Eridu was founded, but are still active in the present day. Due to their lack of biological needs, constructs can be without power for years or even decades and then be reactivated without much issue, as happened with Qingyi, Banyue and Aria, all of whom entered in to some form of stasis or shutdown before being reactivated much later in time.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 历史记录

^zzz-13025-6

> The methods to create Intelligent Constructs trace back to the "Old Civilization" before the founding of Eridu. They have remained as citizens of Eridu for decades. In the past, "Intelligent Construct Threat Theory" was a prevalent theory that, given some intelligent constructs were originally produced as weapons, and even those that weren't were often physically and mentally more proficient than humans in many areas, that their very existence posed a threat to humanity. This theory is said to have "died out" around fifty years ago, and in the modern day, constructs are almost entirely normalized in society, with very few if any people having issues with them. They also are able to integrate into human-centric families, such as the construct Vince having a human daughter he adopted.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-13025-7

> Constructs that have passed the Forbidden Fruit Test are fully recognized as legal citizens of New Eridu, along with humans and Thirens. Constructs have full autonomy over their bodies, and the ability to dismantle them is illegal, to the point that even Public Security would only do so if the risk to civilians was too great, and all other options had been exhausted.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-13025-8

> Despite this, constructs still face challenges in everyday life. Some humans still have reservations towards constructs as a whole. Banyue was discriminated against, making some people regard him as nothing more than a killing machine, while Vesper had her Test certification illegally locked away, allowing TOPS Entertainment to suppress her intelligence and strip her of her autonomy, as on paper she could be considered "property".〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
