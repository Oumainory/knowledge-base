> In the past, the Night Horror fought against the ancestors of the Nightwatchers in a battle that lasted several days. Though it ultimately fell, it used its power to place a curse upon the people. The Night Horror began haunting their dreams, such that, when they would fall asleep, the Ethereal would sometimes call to them, making them sleepwalk into Hollows, only regaining their wits at the moment before they succumb to corruption and transform into Ethereals themselves. This curse grew stronger the more people fell asleep, and so the afflicted people moved to the Outer Ring and began to live a nocturnal lifestyle, founding a tribe known as the "Nightwatchers" in the process.
