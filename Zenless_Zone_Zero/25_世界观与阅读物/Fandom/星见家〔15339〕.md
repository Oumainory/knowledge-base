> The Hoshimi family rose to fame through martial prowess and the power of the cursed katana Tailless, forged by the first head of the family. The third-generation head of the family, "Swordmaster", became a member of the first generation of Void Hunters, and several other heads of the family made significant contributions to the city. They were exceptional and unique warriors, with them upholding the family's reputation. However, after a certain point, they stopped prioritizing martial skills and involved themselves in the city management using their political power: it was with Hoshimi Miyabi, the youngest Void Hunter in New Eridu, that the trend got inverted, with her becoming a great warrior without relying on the power of Tailless; the only moment where she had lost control of the katana was as a kid, during the Fall of the Old Capital, after she had to use it to kill her corrupted mother out of mercy.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
