### 历史记录

^zzz-3581-2

> Sunbringer was originally born as an Ethereal within the Hive Lord Hollow. She grew to become an Enchainer Larva, and challenged the current Hive Lord, but was defeated. Despite this, she didn't die, and managed to claim a portion of the Hive Lord's power. Using this, she was able to leave the Hollow, barely surviving with the power she had seized. On the verge of death, she was found by Arche, who had already founded Helios Academy at this time. He took her back to his academy, named her "Sunbringer" and took her on as a protégé.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3581-3

> During this time, Sunbringer saw people in Eridu and the Academy, and wanted to live alongside them, believing this world was better than the cut-throat world of the Hollow. Her desire to live among humanity made her unconsciously draw on her Genesis Reconstruction powers, giving herself a human form. Arche taught her how to control her instincts as an Ethereal, and told her to keep her true identity secret. She would go on to collaborate with Beliora, Remielle, "Swordmaster", "Joyous", and the Leader of the Falkenhayn Mercenary Troupe to retake the world from the Hollows, destroying all of the Enchainers and seizing their Karma Cores. For their achievements, these seven individuals would be granted the title of Void Hunter. Despite this, the Great Hollows were unable to be destroyed, and so for Sunbringer, who had become the Enchainer of the Hive Lord Hollow, she chose to actively suppress it, shrinking it down and keeping it under control with her power alone.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3581-4

> In her brief time with humanity, she would achieve many things. She founded the city of Roscaelifer, and went on to invent Bangboo, with her prototype for the robots helping Joyous during his navigation of the Divine Maze. She had the notion to create even more intelligent Bangboo who were better at protecting humanity, with Lockspring being the first creation on her path to this idea. She built into him sophisticated combat systems and programmed him with the directive to protect humanity, though ultimately this would drive him into the hands of The Creator after the Fall of the Old Capital. With the help of the other Void Hunters, she would work on the creation of Rosetta Data, secretly using the Hive Lord Hollow as its foundation.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3581-5

> At some point in time, Sunbringer led expeditions into the Lemnian Hollow, discovering, naming, and coordinating the mining of Lumite Ore to be turned into Porcelume for the sake of the people's future. The people looked to her with a mix of reverence and fear during this time, due to her incredible, almost inhuman skill at navigating Hollows, and her refusal to explain how she gained such insight. She later came into contact with the Creator, who stole a portion of her power, which in the far future would be reflected in Their messenger's form.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3581-6

> Using the power of Porcelloy to seal a Karma Core within it, Flint Workshop would create the Void Hunter Weapons, which Sunbringer and Arche would experiment with as part of their Project Sunseeker, which involved fusing a Void Hunter with the Karma Core in their weapon, transforming them into an artificial Enchainer and granting them even greater power to fight against the Hollows while also avoiding the side effects of using the weapon. This ultimately failed, and resulted in Beliora Vike transforming fully into an Enchainer and becoming a threat to humanity until Remielle slew her.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3581-7

> Ultimately, Sunbringer's act of suppressing the Hive Lord Hollow went against her instincts as an Enchainer, and she took measures to ensure that it would remain suppressed for as long as possible, while denying herself the capacity to become a threat to humanity. She divided her Karma Core into three, creating Eldreda, Lindverne and Dracaene in the process. This left her as an empty shell, an "Enchainer who was no longer an Enchainer," and would normally have caused her body to mutate, but she also forged shackles from Porcelloy, entombing herself in the form of a statue and becoming the kindling for the Rosetta Data.<ref name="who" />〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> Though only a shell of herself, Sunbringer's consciousness remained as she continued to source Rosetta Data from her Hollow. She described herself as a "sleepwalker," aware of the happenings throughout Roscaelifer in a subconscious state. In the present era of New Eridu, after The Creator descended upon the Lemnian Hollow due to The Exaltists' efforts, all of the Great Hollows saw spikes in activity, including the Hive Lord Hollow, which threw the Rosetta Data into chaos temporarily. It was at this time that Eldreda came to the room that Sunbringer had sealed herself within, and learned the truth of the matter.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
