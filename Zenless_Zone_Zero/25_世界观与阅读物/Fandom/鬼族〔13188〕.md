> Oni are a race of humanoids with demonic traits. They have red, blue, or yellow skin, pointed ears, demonic tails, fangs, black-tipped horns extruding from their foreheads, and white hair. The lifespan of an Oni seems to be much longer than that of an average human, but with a slower maturity speed.
