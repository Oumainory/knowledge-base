### 历史记录

^zzz-41005-2

> Angel worked as a researcher in anti-corruption medicines, alongside her partner Wayland. The two had daughter, Yidhari, who was born ten years before the Fall of the Old Capital. During her time as a researcher she and Wayland managed to save the lives of Captain Morgan and his crew from ether corruption. As a result, everyone in Port Elpis was incredibly friendly to the Murphy family. When Yidhari was seven, Angel and Wayland began working under TOPS at the Darkwater Research Facility in the Outer Ring, which required them to be away from home for weeks at a time, rarely seeing Yidhari over the course of the next three years. Unbeknownst to the pair, TOPS' interest in anti-corruption medicine was to serve their own purposes of bolstering and empowering their own private forces, and the necessity of staying so long away from home was because the work being done was illegal. When this came to light, Angel and Wayland resolved to end their research and return home to make up for the time they lost with Yidhari.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> On Angel's birthday, Yidhari tried to give her a present, but was too late to catch the boat that took them from Port Elpis to the facility, so Grandpa Gu took her there himself. The family reunited there, but it was on that day that Hollow Zero passed the Zenless Limit, and its activity caused the nearby Outer Ring Hollow to swallow the facility. With TOPS' executives having taken all of the safety gear to save themselves, the Murphys were left stranded in the Hollow with no way out. To make matters worse, Yidhari, who was already more vulnerable to corruption being a child, inhaled ether particles that accelerated her own corruption.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> While Wayland uses his old Proxy skills to navigate a path out of the Hollow, Angel worked to stabilize Yidhari's condition, injecting her with an experimental serum of her own design to stave off the corruption. Wayland would ultimately die having used so much anti-corruption medicine that it shortened his lifespan, but he found a route for Angel and Yidhari to escape. The pair followed it, but the Dead End Butcher had made its way in to the Hollow, and began pursuing them. Exhausted, in bad shape from the corruption, and recognizing that the nearby Rift that would let them escape was only big enough for her daughter to escape through, she told Yidhari to keep going, promising she'd be right behind her, but instead turned to distract the Ethereal, luring it away.
