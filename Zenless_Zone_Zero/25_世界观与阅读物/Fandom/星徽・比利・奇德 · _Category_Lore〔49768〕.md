### 历史记录

^zzz-49768-4

> Found in the Outer Ring's Lost Tech Ruins by a young Appon and his mentor, this Intelligent Tactical Unit (or Battle Android) would roam with these drifters until being sold to the leader of "Calydon," who gave him the role of champion and a name: "Billy Kid." Billy's time as the "Red Scarf" of Calydon made him infamous, with thugs cowering in fear upon hearing his name, earning him the name of "Calydon Reaper". He also became close with fellow member Big Daddy and his owner's daughter Caesar King, the latter whom he called "Big Sis."〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-49768-5

> When Calydon's leader vanished and the gang split, Billy would stay with Big Daddy and Caesar, his spare arm becoming a prosthetic for the latter, who lost her left arm during the chaos. With his owner gone, Billy was an aimless combat weapon once more. After a discussion with the recently formed Sons of Calydon, they decided to let Billy leave. Before his departure, he would form a close bond with Lighter, his successor-to-be. Billy also received two guns from a friend before he left.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-49768-6

> With a taste of freedom, Billy ventured to New Eridu, making the city his new home. By some "weird twist of fate," Billy ended up with the Cunning Hares, and in turn became a close ally to the Legendary Proxy Phaethon, assisting them countless times during the story. During the events of New: Eridan Sunset (B), Billy, with money from Nicole, was able to buy some modifications that would transform him into something like a Starlight Knight.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-49768-15

> 比利·奇德，是一台通过了禁果测试的智能构造体。简言之，拥有自我意志的机器人。
> 人如其名，虽然有着机器人的外表，他的内心却（幼稚的）像个孩子。
> 钟爱一部名为「星徽骑士」的特摄片。希望自己也能成为剧中主角那样的英雄，于是总是以星徽骑士自居，还会用较为夸张的姿态模仿剧中台词。
> 但这样的行为似乎只能造成一些（适合用来调节气氛的）喜剧效果。
> 「不要把我说的好像笨蛋一样啊！我明明是很聪明的！」——by比利
