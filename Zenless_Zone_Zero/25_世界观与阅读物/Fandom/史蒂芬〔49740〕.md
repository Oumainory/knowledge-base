### 历史记录

^zzz-49740-3

> Originally hailing from Waifei Peninsula, Stephen came from a poor background, barely scraping by in Sailume Bay. He came to obtain a pet mouse he named Meister, and became inspired by them, eventually becoming a food critic. His popularity grew with time, and he eventually came to try out the hot pot from Simmer, but was told by the owner Blaze that pets weren't allowed in the restaurant or in the outdoor eating areas. While waiting for their order, Stephen took Meister in to the nearby alley, but Blaze had recently disinfected it, and though she tried to warn them Meister ingested the disinfectant and died as a result; Meister's death enrages Stephen, becoming the catalyst for his actions following this, with him swearing revenge on Simmer.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-49740-4

> Stephen's popularity grew exponentially, and he became the star of a TV show titled NEBC: Infernal Connoisseur, where he became well known for his biting and scathing reviews of various restaurants. He visited Simmer again, and was appalled by the food selection, heavily criticizing Blaze's work, and challenging her, stating that the loser of the challenge would have to retire from their profession. This prompted Blaze to close Simmer for a short while as she started reworking her dishes, getting help from various parties, including the Angels of Delusion, Belobog Heavy Industries, Yunkui Summit, New Eridu Public Security, and Phaethon.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-49740-5

> During this time, Stephen was caught in a Hollow, and suffered from corruption. To alleviate his symptoms, he was prescribed a medicine called CorruClean, though he had to manage it carefully as eating various foods after taking the medicine caused side effects. When he returned to sample the food, he was astounded by the quality, and praised Blaze for her work even though he still planned to post a negative review of Simmer out of spite. However after hearing of her resolve in the face of losing her taste due to the same medicine he was taking, Stephen retracts his negative review and accepts defeat. He also elaborated on the side effects of CorruClean to Blaze and everyone present, who had all been unaware of these effects and explained much of Blaze's rash behavior over the entire period.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-49740-6

> Reflecting on his loss at the riverside path, Stephen encounters Jane Doe who was looking for Cissia; upon seeing Jane he believes that Meister had been reincarnated into a rat Thiren. Stephen's antics annoy Jane so much that she handcuffs him to the railing as he spirals into a mental breakdown as passers-by look on and wonder what was happening; he is left handcuffed for some time, but is eventually freed. Following this, Stephen formally announces his retirement from being a food critic, instead taking Blaze's words to heart and wanting to create "something delicious with friends" and becomes an investor in Simmer. He also offered to fully pay for all rat Thirens who dined at Simmer - a promotional campaign that only further boosts the restaurant's popularity.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
