### 历史记录

^zzz-3982-9

> Before being known as Soldier 11 of Obol Squad, she was originally a replica named Harin from Silver Squad, a name she deemed "too weak" for her. Silver Squad was a company that consisted of an infantry made up of replicas, artificial humans made via genetic cloning. These replicas were incredibly skilled and loyal soldiers who could be easily replaced. Silver Squad was led by Soldier 0 - Anby, who was considered to be the best of the replicas when it came to combat prowess.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3982-10

> However, despite Silver Squad being considered a game changer, the costs to maintain and produce replicas of high-caliber was too expensive. Alongside being part of an internal power struggle, the Defense Force decided it was best to keep just one of the replicas and dispose of the rest. Due to her skills, Anby was the one they were going to keep, but she soon began purposefully failing  tasks in an attempt to save her 'sisters' from disposal. In the end, Obsidian Division devised a suicide mission for Silver Squad, keeping Anby's name off the deployment list as well.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3982-11

> The night prior to the mission, Harin was training in preparation for the upcoming missions, during which overexerted herself and suffered an injury. Noticing what her 'sister' had done, Anby took her place on the mission meant to kill all but her, whilst Harin rested. Anby would watch her all her squadmates supposedly die, buried by rubble and leaving an injured Anby to return alone.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3982-12

> Upon her return, Anby was ordered to kill Soldier 11, an order she refused to follow by slitting her throat in an act of disloyalty. With that, Silver Squad was disbanded and Anby's body was disposed of in the Old Capital, where she was later found alive by Nicole Demara.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3982-13

> Harin would not know the truth of what happened until years later. In her mind, Anby had abandoned her and the rest of Silver Squad. Feeling betrayed by one she greatly admired and left to deal with an awful situation by herself, Harin began to resent Anby, setting her sights on becoming stronger than her. It was around this time she finally gave up the name Harin and fully went by Soldier 11.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3982-14

> Over time, Soldier 11 would become an exemplary soldier, receiving promotions and eventually becoming the final member of Obol Squad, a division with the goal of finding out what truly happened on the day Eridu fell. Upon joining Obol Squad, Soldier 11 was quick to notice Seed's bond with Seed Sr., despite not being told about Obol Squad's agreement to hide Seed Sr.'s death from the pilot, and she began to use the Intelligent Construct's phone to send her texts.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3982-15

> After a mole was discovered within the Defense Force, Soldier 11 was tasked with pretending to defect from the force in an attempt to make contact with the mole and uncover the rebel force they were a part of and disperse them. Learning that Mr. Mole was an admirer of Legendary Proxy - Phaethon, she sent out a commission for a proxy to imitate Phaethon as a way to get closer to him. As fate would have it, the proxy who took up her commission was none other than Phaethon. The duo would complete the mission with the aid of Trigger, Obol Squad's sniper, taking down the rebel faction. Soldier 11 would remain oblivious to Phaethon's identity, believing them to be acting still, despite them revealing the truth.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3982-16

> Sometime during a solo mission, Soldier 11 would wind up as the captive of Twiggy, a strategic mind of Silver Squad and one of her 'sisters' who was assumed to have perished in the suicide mission. She would be saved by Trigger and Anby, who was now a member of the Cunning Hares and going by the last name Demara. During her kidnapping, Soldier 11 sustained heavy injury, leading to temporary retrograde amnesia (something that occurs to replicas under heavy damage).〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### Close

^zzz-3982-22

> Master, based on your firsthand account and Agent Anby's testimony, Soldier 11's real name is Harin. She's one of the clones from New Eridu's former clone soldier project and, like Anby, once served in the Defense Force's Silver Squad.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3982-23

> This research project produced multiple clones programmed to obey the upper ranks of the Defense Force without question. However, due to various complications, it was scrapped years ago. The Defense Force sought to erase the unethical experiment and its results, preserving only one soldier as a symbolic success. Anby had been the intended candidate, but her self-destructive act to protect Soldier 11 led to Soldier 11 being the one preserved. Magus, captain of Obol Squad, later chose to bring Soldier 11 into her squad.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-3982-24

> Having never known the truth, Soldier 11 grew up resenting Anby, feeling abandoned and betrayed. She set her sights on becoming stronger than Anby — stronger than anyone. Still, she developed a strong rapport with her squadmates in Obol Squad, and now considers them her most important allies.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
