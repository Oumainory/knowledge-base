> Eldreda, along with Lindverne and Dracaene, were created by Sunbringer several decades ago, before she later disappeared. Out of all the Sunbringers, Eldreda retained the most memories from the past. She has served as the head of the Administrative Affairs Department since then, with Sunbringer's Intelligent Construct creation Carinus assisting her. He is the one who noted Eldreda's resemblance to Lady Sunbringer. She had some connection to Helios Academy, as Dracaene notes that the Porcelloy used to help Phaethon after they lost control of Helios in their childhood was provided by Eldreda, and she immediately recognized Phaethon as a "Child of Helios", but her connections otherwise are currently unknown.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
