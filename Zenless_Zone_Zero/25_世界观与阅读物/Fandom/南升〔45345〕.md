### 历史记录

^zzz-45345-3

> Nanson's history before he obtained his current position in HAND is unknown. He first appeared after Penny Krueger stole data about The Creator following Ye Shunguang being granted the title of Void Hunter, working with Phaethon after Yanagi had brought them in to help. Nanson primarily provided support via intelligence during the investigation, relaying information about Papago Hollow and Penny's research and helping to coordinate efforts within the Divine Maze. After Penny was apprehended, he decrypted a message from Joyous, allowing Phaethon to see a message he left behind.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-45345-4

> Some time later, Nanson collaborated with Ray Yersin of the White Star Institute, as they followed up on rumors pertaining to the Labyrinth Carrot - Joyous's key to navigating Papago Hollow without Rosetta Data, which had long been thought lost. The two went in to Papago to find it, eventually coming face to face with Phaethon who also was looking for the carrot. After a standoff, the group was chased by an Ulidim through a Fissure, which dropped them in to the derelict Omond Research Station. After learning of the station's state and purpose from D. Ata, Nanson helped coordinate efforts to retrieve the carrot and help Phaethon recover Eous (who had been carrying a fragment of the carrot before being abducted by Ulidim), and worked to bring in outside help from HAND and the HIA to get the research station back up and running.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
