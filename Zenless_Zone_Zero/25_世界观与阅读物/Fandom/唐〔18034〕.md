### 历史记录

^zzz-18034-2

> In his youth, he was a criminal in the same gang as Kay Faelschung, a jewelry counterfeiter. Kay and McDunn came up with a plan to escape the gang: they would hand off the gang's jewels to Public Security in exchange for receiving a lighter sentence. However, their plan to switch the jewels with counterfeits failed, and they were forced to run. They got separated and McDunn waited for Kay to sail to the lighthouse, but an unusual storm struck the lighthouse and broke its light. In desperation, he contacted Public Security for help, but they refused to cooperate as the stolen jewels had not been returned. Kay's boat sank and she ultimately drowned. This incident caused McDunn to become deeply cynical and distrustful of Public Security.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> Years later, McDunn published an article referring to Nautilus Reef as "Sea Crow's Nest," because one of Kay's works — a lamp inscribed with the message "Let its faint glimmer lead you to the sleeping treasure at Sea Crow's Nest." — had been stolen from an art gallery. McDunn deliberately altered the article's date to seem as if it had been published several years prior to the theft, when in fact, McDunn wrote it to lure the criminals to Nautilus Reef. His plan was for the criminals to seek him out for help, as he was familiar with that area, then he would shipwreck their boat and drown them all together. However, Qingyi and Zhu Yuan caught wind of his plan before it came to fruition, and PubSec apprehended the criminals in their base.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

The blue light was actually a signal that connected to a submarine carrying the hidden jewels, which stopped moving near Nautilus Reef, and McDunn operated the lighthouse to guide them through the reef as quickly as possible.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
