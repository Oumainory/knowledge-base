### 历史记录

^zzz-21337-11

> Pan Yinhu had no kin to rely on during his youth due to the scarcity of panda Thirens across New Eridu, and had to scrape by on odd jobs he could find. He often suffered starvation during this period of his life, but eventually he found out his Ether aptitude was decent enough, and his raw physical strength sufficient enough to survive as a Hollow Raider.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-21337-12

> To ensure no one stole what he claimed from the Hollows, Yinhu was willing to fight anything that would get in his way, be they ethereals, gangs or other Hollow raiders. This continued until he had a chance encounter with Yixuan inside of a Hollow. Yixuan was merely lost at the time and wanted to ask for directions, but Yinhu had just recovered an item related to porcelume that he knew would be worth a fortune. Interpreting Yixuan's intent as wanting to steal this item, he immediately fought her, only to be summarily defeated. Exhausted from the fight, and overcome by hunger, he passed out.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-21337-13

> When Pan Yinhu awoke, he found that Yixuan had taken him out of the Hollow, saving his life. She informed him that long-term exposure to Ether was making him struggle with his inner demons, and taught him a training regime to manage his violence and aggression. The effects were immediate, but with hunger still gnawing at him, he asked her if she had any food. Yixuan informed him that Yunkui Summit offers three meals a day. This, combined with the desire to keep hold of the technique she had taught him that had given him peace of mind, Yinhu joined Yunkui Summit. It was only upon joining however that he learned that "three meals a day" meant "funds for three meals a day", and also that there were no cooks working among the mystics. This led to Yinhu learning to cook himself, becoming the primary chef of Yunkui Summit.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-21337-14

> When he joined, Ju Fufu was already training, making her his immediate senior, but Ye Shiyuan and Ye Shunguang arrived to join at the exact same time that he did. Yixuan, rather than deal with any real hastle, decided to pull lots to see who would be the most senior and junior among her three new disciples. Yinhu was the second lot pulled, making him junior to Shiyuan and senior to Shunguang.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-21337-17

> 潘引壶，云岿山门下弟子，随便观的首席大厨兼财务管理人，使得一手好拳法，精通点穴术。
> 作为随便观里管钱的人，他深谙节流生财之道，5000丁尼的肉他能讲价到500丁尼，能在家自己做出来的物品他绝不会到外面购买，各大超市商店的优惠券他都能随手从身上掏出来。
> 拜他所赐，随便观的成员每天都能吃到美味而实惠的饭菜。作为掌勺大厨，他对于菜品的口味要求严格，且高度重视同伴们的评价。
> 此外，推测由于他长期的省钱行为，无论是随便观的总经费还是他自己的私人小金库，其数额都已经积攒到了能让人两眼一直的程度。

### Close

^zzz-21337-20

> 作为放眼整个新艾利都也极为罕见的熊猫希人，潘引壶自儿时起便无任何亲人帮衬，独自在新艾利都摸爬滚打。没真正被饿过的人不会知道饥饿有多可怕，为了混口饭吃，潘引壶把自己能尝试的职业都做了一遍，最终因以太适性良好而成为了盗洞客。
> 成为盗洞客的他因一次偶然遇到了仪玄，在被暴打及点化后选择拜入云岿山门下，通过修炼术法净化自己日渐暴戾的心性。
> 由于拜入门下的时间较晚，他的师弟师妹实际上很少，主人，您就是其中之一，因此他会用给您开小灶、塞给您商店优惠券等方式来表达身为师兄对您的关爱。
