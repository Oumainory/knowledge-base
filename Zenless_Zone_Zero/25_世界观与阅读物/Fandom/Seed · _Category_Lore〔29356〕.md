### 历史记录

^zzz-29356-7

> The history of the young girl who'd become the heavy weapons specialist of Obol Squad is partially clouded in mystery still. However, her story began at the same place as Phaethon's: Helios Academy.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-29356-8

> There she was subjected to experiments that were likely responsible for her unique ability to control, disable or even destroy machinery with her mind, alongside causing several "defects" in her personality, leading to her being emotionally disconnected to the concept of life. It's hard to ascertain what really happened as Hollow Zero's sudden expansion brought the fall of the Old Capital and Helios Academy with it. She also suffered a form of retrograde amnesia, making it difficult for her to remember what happened.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-29356-9

> This girl would remain in Hollow Zero for half a month, miraculously showing no signs of Ether corruption. She was found at the Helios Academy monument by a mass-produced military Intelligent Construct unit named Seed. Seed was tasked with searching for survivors within Hollow Zero, but upon meeting this girl, the "Iron Order" program: a defense-system installed in the logic-cores of these units that would stop them from disobeying orders, recognized her abnormal powers and classified her as a "extremely high-risk target", with Seed's autonomy being overridden to ensure the threat was eradicated. Somehow, Seed's own personality was able to fight back and disabled the "Iron Order" program, at the cost of greatly damaging his logic-core.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-29356-10

> Returning from the Hollow, Defense Force medic Veronica performed a check-up on her and noted how she "has no heart," in reference to how she perceives and comprehends life. Despite this, Seed took this orphan in and named her Flora, becoming a father figure to this mysterious girl and teaching her how to have a heart. Seed also began investigating into the fall of the old capital, hoping to find the truth about his  adopted daughter.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-29356-11

> A while after taking Flora in Seed's self-damaging of his logic core began to show effects, with him shutting down for extended periods of time and having only a few months to live. He kept Flora oblivious to the fact he was dying, explaining his periods of down-time as "falling asleep." As a precaution, he joined Obol Squad with the ulterior motive of taking care of Flora after he died and to keep his death a secret from her, this motive was prematurely revealed after Flora's powers caused several mechs to go berserk before self-destructing.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-29356-12

> Knowing how Flora liked his cockpit, Seed decided to teach her how to pilot him, so she could take care of herself when he was "sleeping." On the first piloting, Flora asked if this meant she would be Seed, which the Intelligent Construct agreed on, leading to his codename being inherited by her, with him now going by the name Seed Sr. The two Seeds would partake in missions together, until Seed Sr.'s logic core finally failed and he passed on. Before his death, he recorded a message for Seed and entrusted coordinates to where they first met to Veronica. Seed Sr. died with no regrets, except for not being able to see her fully grow up, one of his final thoughts was wondering if the seeds he planted where they first met had become a field of flowers by now.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-29356-13

> It was unanimously agreed by Obol Squad to tell Seed the false narrative that Seed Sr. was on a special mission and wouldn't be able to talk or be active for a long while. This lie was strengthened by Soldier 11, who despite not being aware of the situation, recognized how much Seed Sr. meant to her, using his phone to send her texts to her.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-29356-14

> Seed would be present for the incidents involving Porcelumex's co-CEO, Ferox colluding with the Exaltists and the eradication of the Exaltists in Waifei Peninsula. Afterwards, Seed established a safe house in Hollow Zero, as she contemplated methods to bring Seed Sr. back from his dormancy. The resulting events led to her confronting Veronica, and in the ensuing incident, she discovered that he was truly gone. Veronica gave Seed the coordinates Seed Sr. entrusted her with, which Phaethon and Fairy used to locate the spot in Hollow Zero where they had first met. Seed brought Seed Sr. to the monument, now surrounded by flowers that had bloomed in the 11 years since they were first planted, with her tears activating a mechanism in him that then played his final message to her, allowing her to finally process the grief of his death.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-29356-15

> His final message now imparted to Seed, she has now decided to continue in his footsteps, to try and uncover the mysteries behind the Fall of the Old Capital and her own past, that Seed Sr. had worked towards in his final days. Seed also learnt she was from Helios Academy, but is currently unable to remember anything else.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-29356-18

> 「席德」，新艾利都防卫军·黑曜石营·奥波勒斯小队的火炮手。本名芙罗拉，「席德」这一代号继承自其监护人、防卫军战斗用智能构造体「席德」，后者则已将代号变更为「老席德」。
> 
> 根据多方证言，「席德」是「老席德」于旧都陷落时，从零号空洞内营救出的、身份不明的孤儿，「老席德」收养了她并在防卫军宿舍内独自将其抚养成人。
> 
> 距今数年前，「老席德」通过申请成功编入奥波勒斯小队，随后不久便因逻辑核心的不可逆损坏而死亡，「席德」也因此接替他成为奥波勒斯小队的成员。当前我们所能听到的「老席德」语音，均为其自带的系统预载语音。
> 
> 在机械改造与研究方面，「席德」具有远超常人的天赋，可以设计并制造出许多常人无法想象的发明；同时，她具备超高的以太适性，并能以驾驶、远程操作、加装战斗模组等方式将「老席德」的战斗力发挥至极致。
> 
> 因此尽管体能训练严重不达标、身份来历全是谜团，军方还是默许了接收她作为一名精英军人服役。
> 
> 日常中的「席德」行为模式异于常人，她以「老席德」的驾驶舱为休憩的固定地点，喜好钻进狭窄的地方，极度反感与同伴以外的人进行接触交谈，且言行的逻辑跳跃性极强，具有很高的沟通难度。
> 
> 幸运的是，主人，不知为何，她对您和助手二号表现出异常的亲近，您既省了取得她信任与喜欢的冗长流程，也不用担心她不时表现出的危险破坏性会波及到您。可喜可贺。
> 
> 提议：多以花朵、机器人等该代理人最喜欢的事物作为沟通话题，用平常心看待其对话中无法理解逻辑或含义的部分，可以有效拉近您与她的关系。

^zzz-29356-21

> 「席德」自幼便存在某些诡异甚至危险的异能力表现，具体现象为：可以操纵、瘫痪甚至引爆一定范围内的机械造物；此能力仅在其陷入严重的情绪不稳定状态时会出现，其外在表现为双眼部位闪烁红光。
> 
> 正是由于此能力的存在，令初次发现她的「老席德」的自警系统将其自动识别为高危对象，并间接造成了「老席德」逻辑核心的不可逆损毁。
> 
> 同时，「席德」被「老席德」从零号空洞带出时，便已经存在人格层面上的缺陷，因不明原因，她缺失了对生命的理解、感知与共情力。
> 
> 在被收养的几年间，「老席德」一直致力于培养其完整的人格，他去世后该接力棒由奥波勒斯小队的众人接过，时至今日，「席德」的人格缺陷已得到显著的改善。
> 
> 主人，根据您与「席德」之前于零号空洞的探索记录，可知「席德」的真实身份与您类似，都是来自赫利俄斯机关的学徒。
> 
> 推定她在赫利俄斯机关期间，所隶属的研究项目与您不同，很可能是某个与机械研究相关的项目，上述「席德」的异能力与人格缺陷，都是该项目的遗留结果；由于代理人遗失与赫利俄斯机关以及旧都陷落相关的记忆，目前尚无法解析出更进一步的信息。
> 
> 此外，主人，她有时候会微笑着一直注视着我，推定可能有将我拆解以满足好奇心的冲动，十分瘆人。严正要求对我的人身安全施加多重保护，以免您痛失一名可爱的得力助手。
