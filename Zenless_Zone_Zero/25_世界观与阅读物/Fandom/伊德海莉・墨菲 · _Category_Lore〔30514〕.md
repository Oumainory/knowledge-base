### 历史记录

^zzz-30514-4

> Yidhari Murphy was born to Wayland; a man with aspirations of becoming a Proxy, but later entered the field of Ether science as to pursue his future wife and mother of Yidhari: Angel. Yidhari Murphy was a notably healthy child who spent most of her time under the watchful eyes of Grandpa Gu, whilst her parents worked at Darkwater Research Center, a TOPS-funded facility that was accesible via Port Elpis which focused on the research of Ether Corruption medicine.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-30514-5

> Wayland and Angel hoped that their work would be for the betterment of humanity, but were aware that the experimental drugs they were creating were being used to enhance TOPS's private security forces illegally. After three years of working at Darkwater, the octopus Thiren couple decided to leave so they could spend time with their daughter. Their final day was to coincide with Angel's birthday.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-30514-6

> On the day of the Old Capital's fall, Yidhari, now ten years old, planned to give her mom a birthday gift before she left, but missed the boat due to being denied entry to the port. Upon seeing this, Grandpa Gu took Yidhari personally to Darkwater Research Centre, where she reunited and spent time with her parents. Shortly after, the rapid expansion of Hollow Zero begun and caused several other Hollows to expand as well, including the Outer Ring Hollow, which consumed the facility.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-30514-7

> Whilst trying to find a way out of the Hollow, Yidhari became severely corrupted due to the inhalation of countless Ether particles. The Murphy family also learnt that TOPS had fled with all the protective equipment, thus leaving them stranded in the Hollow. Wayland would map out a path out of the Hollow, while Angel was able to stablise their daughter with the aid of an experimental serum the two were working on. To counteract the effects of corruption, Wayland took excessive dosages of anti-corruption medicine. This had the effect of greatly cutting his lifespan and would lead to his death within the Hollow, but not before he was able to fully map out an escape route, considering his birthday gift to Angel, and saying goodbye to her and their daughter over a radio.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-30514-8

> The mother and daughter then followed the path Wayland had found, but found themselves pursued by the infamous Dead End Butcher. Nearing the exit, Yidhari was told by her mother to continue and that she'll be right behind her, only for Angel to guide the Butcher away from her daughter, allowing Yidhari to escape, at the cost of both her parents life. Yidhari would be found outside the Outer Ring Hollow by then Overlord Pompey and Mors of The Vanquishers, who took her to Blazewood and was later returned to the safety of Grandpa Gu. Yidhari would go onto repress the events of that fateful day, which put huge strain on her psyche.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-30514-9

> Over the ten-year gap between Eridu's fall and the events of Zenless Zone Zero, Yidhari Murphy developed a unique ability thanks to the the experimental serum of her parents. This ability took the form of unwilling retrocognition, being able to see past events that occurred within the Hollows. This power was difficult to control, as it skewed her perceptions and dulled her emotions while in Hollows, but she persisted and it would become an intergral tool for her as she became a Proxy. One day, whilst browsing Inter-Knot, she stumbled upon a story about a protagonist who could read the story of Hollows. Following the link therein took her to the Spook Shack forum, where Yuzuha explained that she had made up the story, but after Yidhari revealed she had powers similar to said protagonist, she was immediately invited to Spook Shack and joined under the name 'Strawberry Parfait.'〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-30514-10

> During a mission within the Lemnian Hollow, Yidhari's ability was mutated by the Miasma within, allowing her the ability to rematerialize these past events, but causing a greater toll on her psyche and made her unable to distinguish the faces of people, becoming interchangeable with images of the past she saw in the Hollow. Sarah, current Overseer of The Exaltists learnt of Yidhari's newly-found ability and hired the octopus Thiren to help them on a mission. She manipulated her into rematerializing a town that was located in Timesworn Hills in order to find a door that supposedly led to The Creator. Being within the proximity of The Creator cursed Yidhari to be imprisioned inside the newly-restored town's Auditorium, Miasma binding her body and forcing her into a deep slumber.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-30514-11

> Overtime, countless people would find the town and subsequently become trapped as well, their bodies cocooned in Miasmic flora as their deep regrets took form as alternate versions of themselves named Drowned Ideals. Exaltist member Viggo Moss, whose own Drowned Ideal kept watch of Yidhari and ensured she didn't get corrupted, took charge of the Drowned Ideals as they began to populate the town. Yidhari was able to awaken several times, but anytime she tried to leave the curse warped the space of the Hollow around her, preventing her escape. When she would inevitably have to sleep again, with the Wandering Hunter would come to return her to the auditorium.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-30514-12

> Yidhari was finally able to break the curse with the help of Legendary Proxy Phaethon, and Komano Manato and Lucia Elowen of Spook Shack, who were trying to find a Lumite Ore mining site before Porcelumex did, only to stumble across the Town. Phaethon's ocular implants was able to sever the Miasma which bound Yidhari, and also allowed her to see the Proxy clearly, unlike anyone else in the Hollow. The trio, along with the help of Banyue, Krampus Compliance Authority and the Drowned Ideal of Manato were able to free Yidhari and everyone else imprisoned by the town. Yidhari would later assist in laying the Wandering Hunter to rest.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-30514-13

> Afterwards, Yidhari underwent psychological therapy within the Echo Chamber, the old clinic of Dr. Lohr that Porcelumex had lease and renovated to assist in the recovery of those within the town. During a session with psychatrist Lydia, in regards to her past, Yidhari entered a deep coma due to the strain caused from repressing her memories. Phaethon, Manato and Lucia would assist Yidhari with overcoming the trauma of her past, via the usage of VR technology courtesy of the H.I.A. After several dives into her dream and slaying the Dead End Butcher within them, Yidhari awoke and was finally able to return to her normal life, able to write tales on Spook Shack and spend time with her new friends.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-30514-16

> 伊德海莉，除了代理人的身份外，也兼任绳匠以及怪啖屋的投稿作者。
> 在其他人看来，伊德海莉在寻常情况下待人处事总有种闲适的慵懒感，不过就记录的资料来判断，相较于慵懒这样的描述，她更像在被一种与周遭环境格格不入的虚无感所包裹。
> 或许对多数人而言，这是沉浸创作之人的通病——作为天赋异禀的怪诞故事作者，伊德海莉应当比其他人更容易沉浸在自己构造的幻想世界中。
> 可实际上，她的异常表现更多是源自自身能力带来的负担。
> 她在空洞中能接触到常人难以想象的庞大信息，那些信息纠缠着空洞中长年累月堆积的过往。而这无数过往积累的负荷不断冲击着她对现实的感知，最终连同情感也在冲刷下逐渐变得迟钝。
> 能够承受这份负担，将其变成自己的能力之余还能用自己的方式正常生活，便是伊德海莉超越常人的天赋。

^zzz-30514-19

> 伊德海莉对雨天与泡澡有着非同一般的热爱。
> 因为这两样喜好能在现实的世界带给她短暂的安宁与平静，让她能够享有片刻闲暇，不再为脑海中承载的纷杂信息流困扰。
> 
> 然而她的这两样热爱在最近的相处中发生了些许改变。
> 她不再习惯孤独，不再满怀忐忑与期盼地追逐雨天，不再依靠将身体浸泡在温水中疗愈自我。
> 她开始在晴朗的天空下漫步，她能从容地穿过熙攘的人群…她开始期待尝试一切对她而言崭新的事物。
> 
> 而伊德海莉这一切改变的伊始，源自与您的相遇。
