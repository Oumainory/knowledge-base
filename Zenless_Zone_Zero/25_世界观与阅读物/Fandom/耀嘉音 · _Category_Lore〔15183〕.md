### 历史记录

^zzz-15183-8

> Like many of the citizens living in New Eridu, Astra Yao was but a child when Eridu fell due to Hollow Zero. Having been missing for 24 hours since the disaster, Astra was found between the border of Vulcan and Janus Quarters by a H.I.A squad. The captain of the squad helped calm Astra by teaching her a song as she readied her for extraction, whilst the rest of her team looked for other missing people, but only two members returned, the rest having been slain by Ethereals. With no other option, the captain told Astra to sing the song she just taught her, before ordering that only the rope connected to Astra should be lifted at full speed. Astra Yao was saved, but at the cost of the captain and her team. The final words of her savior were "Be happy, child, live on with a smile."〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15183-9

> Whilst at a makeshift shelter, wondering if her parents were okay, Astra overheard the music of Joran de Winter on a speaker. Her fear and loneliness melted away thanks to the melody, and the tears she once wept in sadness were now tears of joy. Noticing that others in the shelter were moved by Joran's music, Astra knew that the new era would need a new songstress and decided to become exactly that.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15183-10

> Astra's start in the world of music was rough, with no access to good recording facilities or the funds to obtain a decent master copy, Astra's first album "Stars of Lyra" sold partially well. It wasn't until she signed with Odeum HAE that her music career blossomed. Astra Yao soon became one of the most popular musicians in all of New Eridu, to the point she obtained significant roles in the movies Family, The Heartbeat, "Streetlamp Princess," and appeared in countless forms of commercials for brands like Carlishe. It got to the point rival music license Fugue sent Evelyn Chevalier to spy on her, which ended with the two becoming good friends to the point several of Astra's fans have shipped them.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

^zzz-15183-11

> One day, an Intelligent Construct entered a coma after partaking in a beta test for a new VR machine that was funded by the TOPS Alliance, having learnt TOPS swept that info under the rug and with no way to openly oppose them, Astra used her connections to find a skilled VR-enthusiast to save the Intelligent Construct. She also used a prototype consciousness synchronizer with her Bangboo Snap in order to directly speak with the chosen enthusiast which just so happened to be Phaethon. Since this encounter, Astra Yao became close friends with the proxy siblings, Belle and Wise, having them assist her in ditching a public event and thwarting an act of terrorism at her New Year's concert.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

### 代理人详情

^zzz-15183-18

> 耀嘉音是公认的「丽都第一歌姬」，凭借其超凡的嗓音、舞台感染力和创作才华，引领了整个时代的音乐风潮。「如果音乐是灵魂的共鸣，那耀嘉音就是点燃这一切的火花。」某知名乐评人曾如此评价她。
> 
> 尽管耀眼如斯，嘉音却保持着难得的真诚与纯粹。舞台上，她的歌声直抵人心。这种特质，让她得以拥有庞大的忠实粉丝群「加饭」，当然嘉音的光芒，也离不开经纪人伊芙琳的悉心守护。而舞台之下，似乎只是一个在生活上依赖着伊芙琳，在空洞里依赖着哲和铃的可爱女孩。
> 
> 「现在挠伊埃斯咯吱窝的话，你会大笑吧？」抱起连接着H.D.D.系统的绳匠，嘉音这样好奇地发问。
> 
> 似乎格外宠爱伊埃斯。
> 思考：人类的情感的确是有趣的课题。嘉音究竟是因为伊埃斯爱屋及乌主人，还是因为主人才爱屋及乌伊埃斯，尚且无法判断。
