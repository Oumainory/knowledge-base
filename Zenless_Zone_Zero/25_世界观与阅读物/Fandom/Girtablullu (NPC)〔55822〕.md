It absorbed her, being able to imitate her form and mannerisms as it tried to become one with the Karma Core fragment inside her. As Phaethon, Billy and Cissia went to reveal their findings about the culprit to Dracaene, Girtablullu used the opportunity to corner and attack Phaethon, while also critically injuring Truvia, though Phaethon was able to escape thanks to Pyrois's intervention.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
