^zzz-6628-3

> Hollow Zero is the oldest, most impactful, and most hazardous Hollow in all of New Eridu. Its expansion was responsible for the destruction of the city of Eridu eleven years prior to the start of the game. Despite the best efforts of its inhabitants, the Hollow expanded relentlessly, engulfing the city and those unable to escape. Though the reason for Hollow Zero's expansion is unknown, Carole Arna, a senior research director of the Helios Academy, has been officially identified as the main culprit, with her name and others involved etched on the pillar of shame. Some people, such as Phaethon and the Obol Squad, believe Carole Arna was innocent and are working to uncover the real truth about what happened.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕

> In a desperate effort to control Hollow Zero, Eridu's leaders detonated fourteen Shiyu Pillars in its path, from the southwest to the northeast. The explosion annihilated any form of matter the Hollow could have fed off of to further expand, creating the Chasm, a massive ravine. This extreme act stopped the disaster and allowed the city to rebuild itself as New Eridu. A memorial was built beside The Chasm to honor the victims of the catastrophe.〔来源叙述待核，未认定为游戏原文〕
